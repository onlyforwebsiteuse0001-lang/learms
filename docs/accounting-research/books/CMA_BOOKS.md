## 1. The finding that matters more than any book list

**ICMAP launched Study Scheme-2025 without permanent study material for its new subjects.**

The institute published a document titled **"Interim Study Resources for New Subjects"**
(`https://www.icmap.com.pk/pdfs/SchemeOfStudy2025/TempStudyResource.pdf`, Tier A). The existence
of this document is an official admission that permanent material for the genuinely new courses —
**O2 Gen. AI & Business Productivity** and **M4 Digital Accounting & Financial Modelling** — was
not ready when the scheme took effect on **16 July 2025**.

**Consequences for students who enrolled in the first cohorts:**
- They are studying from interim resources for a live, examinable subject.
- No established commercial textbook market exists for these two courses, because no publisher
  has had time to build one.
- Past papers cannot exist for a subject that has not been examined under the new scheme.

**Consequence for Learms:** these two subjects are the clearest content gap in Pakistani
accounting education right now. There is no incumbent to displace. If Learms wants a wedge into
the ICMAP market, **O2 and M4 are it** — and the window closes as soon as publishers catch up.

restricted under section 87 of the Companies Act, 2017…". Revising by standard number is
therefore aligned with how the paper is actually constructed.

## 3. ⚠️ The commercial material problem — a warning, not a reading list

**Widely-sold ICMAP preparation material is out of date, and it is not obviously so to a student.**

Material from **Tabani's School of Accountancy** (`tsa.edu.pk`), **Petiwala Books**, and book
lists circulated on blogs such as `mashoorbooks.blogspot.com` was observed listing **legacy
2016/2018 ICMAP subject codes and titles** — for example *"M2 Enterprise Management"* and
*"S3 Business Taxation"*.

Under **Study Scheme-2025** those codes carry different subjects:
- **M2 is Business Taxation** (not Enterprise Management)
- **S2 is Advance Taxation**
- **S3 is Corporate Laws, Governance & Ethics**

**A student buying by code will buy the wrong book.** This also means the task brief's own
subject mapping — which swapped M2 and S2 — matches the *obsolete* material rather than the
current scheme (`../CONTRADICTIONS.md` C-002, C-003).

**Recommendation for Learms:** a "check your book is current" feature keyed to the Scheme-2025
code table would cost almost nothing and prevents a real, common and expensive mistake.

### 4.1 Where the international textbooks fail for a Pakistani student
Every general text above is written for a US or UK context. **For taxation (M2, S2) and corporate
law (M6, S3) they are not merely unhelpful — they are wrong**, because the governing instruments
are Pakistani:

- **Income Tax Ordinance 2001**
- **Sales Tax Act 1990**
- **Companies Act 2017**
- **SECP regulations and circulars**

These are primary sources, published by FBR and SECP, and there is no substitute for them. This
is also the precise point the academic literature makes: Pakistani students are taught from
Anglo-American material about an economy they do not work in
(`../papers/BIGONI_AWAIS_2024_RAT_RACE.md`).

**For Learms this is the clearest content-authoring priority in the CMA syllabus: tax and
corporate law cannot be licensed from an international publisher. They must be written locally.**
