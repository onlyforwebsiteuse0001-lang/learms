## 3. The economic comparison nobody publishes

Standard comparisons list fees side by side. That is the wrong calculation, because **only one
of the three pays you while you train.**

### Net cash position at qualification (Tier E — modelled on Tier-A inputs)

| | CMA (ICMAP, 12-yr route) | CA (ICAP) |
|---|---|---|
| Body fees + mandatory tuition | −727,000 | Lower per paper; tuition separate and **discountable ≥75%** if means-tested |
| Practical training | **−126,600** (6 PTMs) | **+1,300,000 to +1,600,000** (stipend, 30–42 months at regulated minimums) |
| **Net swing vs each other** | | **≈PKR 1.5–2.0 million in CA's favour** |

**This single comparison dominates every other consideration for a financially-constrained
student, and it is essentially absent from Pakistani comparison content.**

### Caveats stated honestly

- CA training requires **securing a training contract**, which is competitive and not
  guaranteed. An ICMAP student who cannot get a CA training place is not choosing between
  these paths.
- CA takes longer in elapsed time and the 2.5-year uninterrupted route **forbids sitting any
  exam during training**.
- The stipend is a floor at the *minimum*; it is still below urban living cost at
  commencement (PKR 27,500/month).
- ICMAP suits people **already employed** who need a part-time route with no placement
  dependency — the flexibility is real and is what the fee buys.

### Where ACCA sits

ACCA's fees are GBP-denominated, so its PKR cost rises with rupee depreciation **independently
of any ACCA price increase**. Against that: it is the only one of the three that travels.
The honest framing is **"pay in hard currency now, buy optionality later"**, not a cost rank.

## 4. The late-entry and batching arbitrage

Three different fee architectures create three different optimal behaviours. Most students
follow none of them because nobody has told them the rules differ.

| Body | Fee architecture | Optimal behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **ACCA** | Flat per exam, but **late entry +125–156%** | **Book on time, every time.** Missing standard entry by ~7 days costs +£249 on an Applied Skills paper. |
| **ICAP** | **Steep marginal discount** — CAF 7,000 first paper, 4,600 each additional | **Batch papers.** 4 CAF papers = 20,800 (5,200/paper) vs 4 separate sittings = 28,000. |
| **ICMAP** | **Flat per course**, no batching discount; coaching/DLP mandatory | No fee arbitrage available. Optimise for **passing first time** — a resit costs the full exam fee, and legacy students hit a **double-fee cliff after six attempts**. |

**Recommendation to Agent 2:** a per-body "fee optimiser" is a small, concrete, genuinely
useful feature. The rules are public, stable, and quantified above.

## 5. Which qualification for which student

Framed as decision rules grounded in the evidence above, not as generic advice.

### Choose **CA (ICAP)** if…
- You can realistically secure a training contract (strong academics, urban, networked).
- You need the qualification to **pay for itself** — the stipend is the decisive factor.
- You intend to practise in Pakistan (audit sign-off, statutory work, regulatory roles).
- You qualify for **CAT–SFS** (family income < PKR 100,000/month, ≥80% HSSC) — this cuts
  tuition ≥75% and registration to PKR 2,000. **This is the single most valuable
  under-claimed benefit in Pakistani accounting education.**
- ⚠️ If you hold MSA-1 or SPM from the old scheme: **Winter 2026 is your last sitting**
  before you are forced onto CFAP-03.

### Choose **ACCA** if…
- **Emigration is a real plan**, not an aspiration. This is the only one of the three that
  transfers.
- You already have income (job or family support) — there is no stipend and no placement.
- You have a degree granting exemptions (but note: exemptions save **time and risk**, not
  money — £114 exemption vs £160 exam).
- You can absorb **GBP currency risk** over 3+ years.

### Choose **CMA (ICMAP)** if…
- You are **already working** and need a part-time route that does not depend on anyone
  hiring you into a training contract. This is ICMAP's genuine, under-stated advantage.
- Your target is **industry cost/management accounting** — manufacturing, FMCG, textiles,
  utilities — rather than audit practice.
- You are entering with a **16-year degree**: at ≈PKR 464,000 and 2 years, the value
  proposition is dramatically better than the 12-year route's ≈PKR 727,000 and 4 years.
- You can fund it without needs-based aid, because **none was found published**.

### Consider **PIPFA first** if…
- Cost is the binding constraint. ICAP cut its PIPFA exemption fee to **PKR 7,400 in 2026**
  (down 45%), making PIPFA → CA the cheapest documented laddered route into chartered status.

### Consider **CMA → CA** if…
- You already hold ICMAP membership: ICAP prices the ICMAP exemption at **PKR 59,200**, a
  defined monetised conversion. Rarely presented as a strategy.

## 6. What cannot be compared, and why that matters

| Dimension | Why comparison fails |
|---|---|
| **Difficulty** | ACCA publishes per-paper pass rates every session. ICMAP publishes none. Comparing "ACCA AAA is 38%" to "ICMAP is moderate" compares a measurement to an opinion. |
| **Completion rates** | No body publishes cohort attrition. Every "X% drop out" claim found in this research was unsourced. |
| **Employment outcomes** | No body publishes graduate destination or salary surveys. |
| **Student population size** | Not published on a comparable basis. The brief's premise that ICMAP students are the most numerous is **unverified** (`../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-005). |

**Recommendation:** Learms should render these as explicit "not published by the institute"
cells rather than omitting the rows. The absence is informative — and the asymmetry in
transparency between ACCA and the two Pakistani bodies is itself one of the more striking
findings of this research.
