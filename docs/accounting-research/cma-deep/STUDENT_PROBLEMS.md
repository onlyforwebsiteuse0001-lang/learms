# ICMAP CMA — Student Problems: Claims Audited Against Evidence

**Area:** 2.3
**Researched:** 2026-09-30

---

## How to read this document

The brief supplied ten grievances attributed to LinkedIn and community reports. Awarding
bodies do not publish their own failure modes, so **Tier-D (community/anecdotal) evidence is
the only evidence that exists for most of these** — and per `../DECISIONS-7.md` D-007, Tier D
can establish *that a grievance is held*, never *that a fact is true*.

Each claim below is therefore given a **verdict**:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed (Tier A)** | Established from ICMAP's own documents |
| **Confirmed by arithmetic** | Follows necessarily from Tier-A inputs |
| **Structurally supported** | ICMAP's own scheme design implies it, even if not stated |
| **Reported, unverified** | Community claim; no independent confirmation found |
| **Partly wrong** | Grievance is real but the stated numbers/units are incorrect |
| **Not found** | No supporting source located |

## Claim 1 — "High fees: modules costing approximately PKR 93,000 each"

**Verdict: Partly wrong — real grievance, wrong unit, and understated for 2026.**

Per-course cost (2025 rates) is PKR 23,000–39,500. Per **level** (3 courses, 6 months) is
PKR 69,000–118,500. The ≈93,000 figure corresponds to **one Managerial level at 2024–25
rates**, not one paper. At current 2026 rates the equivalent is **99,900–102,900 per
managerial level** and **117,000–123,000 per strategic level**.

Full working: `STUDY_SCHEME_2025_COMPLETE.md` §13. Register: `../CONTRADICTIONS.md` C-004.

**The underlying grievance is strongly confirmed** — and worse than usually stated, because
the *total* is what bites: **≈PKR 727,000 minimum for the 12-year entry route**, before books,
travel, or a single resit.

## Claim 2 — "Outdated DLP lectures: students get access to outdated recordings"

**Verdict: Reported, unverified — but structurally plausible and highly consequential.**

No Tier-A or Tier-B source confirms recording age. ICMAP's site provides "Recorded Lectures"
behind a student login, which cannot be inspected without credentials.

**Why it is structurally plausible:** Study Scheme-2025 took effect 16 July 2025 and
introduced two genuinely new courses (O2 Gen. AI & Business Productivity, M4 Digital
Accounting & Financial Modelling). ICMAP itself published an **"Interim Study Resources for
New Subjects under Study Scheme 2025"** PDF — the existence of an *interim* resource is a
Tier-A admission that permanent material for the new subjects was not ready at launch.

Source: https://www.icmap.com.pk/pdfs/SchemeOfStudy2025/TempStudyResource.pdf (linked from
the official syllabus page, accessed 2026-09-30).

**Why it is consequential:** DLP has **no attendance requirement** (Policy Guidelines §8.2),
so working students concentrate there. Material quality in DLP has disproportionate reach.

## Claim 3 — "Study text too lengthy, not updated with current regulations, not effective like ACCA"

**Verdict: Reported, unverified for length; the regulatory-currency concern is structurally real.**

Length and pedagogical quality are subjective and unverified here.

**But regulatory currency is a genuine, structural problem in Pakistan specifically**, and it
is worse for ICMAP than for ACCA for a reason nobody states plainly:

ICMAP's syllabus is anchored to **Pakistani law that changes annually** — the Income Tax
Ordinance and Sales Tax Act are amended every year by the Finance Act, and the Companies Act
2017 is amended periodically. M2 Business Taxation and S2 Advance Taxation are therefore
**structurally guaranteed to go stale every single July**. ACCA's Pakistan-variant tax paper
faces the same problem, but ACCA's approved content providers (BPP, Kaplan) operate on a
commercial per-session republication cycle with an external quality gate. ICMAP produces its
own text in-house with no equivalent external pressure.

**This is the clearest structural argument for the complaint, and it is testable:** compare
the Finance Act year cited in the current ICMAP tax study text against the current tax year.
Learms could surface this automatically. Recommended to Agent 2 in `../RECOMMENDATIONS.md`.

## Claim 4 — "Question bank old: ~50% of questions are already in past papers"

**Verdict: Not found — no source, and the framing may be backwards.**

No source quantifies overlap. The 50% figure has no traceable origin.

**Counter-observation:** for a professional exam, high overlap between the official question
bank and past papers is **normal and often intentional** — it signals the examinable standard.
ACCA, ICAP and CIMA all recycle question *types* deliberately. The complaint only bites if the
overlap makes the bank non-discriminating for *preparation* (i.e. you can pass by memorising
answers without understanding).

**What would make this a real finding** is evidence that ICMAP reuses questions *verbatim
with unchanged numbers*, which is different from reusing question types. That evidence was
not found. Recorded honestly rather than repeated.

## Claim 5 — "Practical training gap: 3 modules offer only ~2 hours of video total, mostly theory"

**Verdict: Reported, unverified on the video quantity — but the structural gap is Confirmed (Tier A).**

The "~2 hours of video" figure cannot be verified without student portal access.

**The structural gap is confirmed from ICMAP's own switchover table**, and it is worse than
the complaint states. Comparing Study Scheme-2018 practical modules to Study Scheme-2025:

| 2018 module | 2025 replacement | What was lost |
|---|---|---|
| PM8 — Applied Analytics with Power BI **& Python** | PTM1 — Data Analytics & Visualisation (Power BI) | **Python dropped from the title** |
| PM1 — **ERP Solutions** and Practical Aspects of Accounting & Auditing Procedures | PTM4 — Audit Procedures | **ERP Solutions dropped from the title** |
| PM2 — Financial Modeling and Management Reporting | *(no direct PTM successor; content largely absorbed into M4)* | Moved from practical module to examined course |
| O4 — Fundamentals of Management | PTM2 — Entrepreneurship and Business Planning | Substantially rescoped |

Source: ICMAP Policy Guidelines Study Scheme-2025 §6 switchover table (Tier A); Study Scheme
2018 (Updated 2023) module list — https://www.icmainternational.com/StudentNotices_PDF/StudyScheme2018_Updated_in_2023_[w.e.f.Fall2023forFeb.2024Exam].pdf

**Finding:** the two most employable hard skills named anywhere in ICMAP's practical
programme — **Python and ERP** — were both removed from module titles in the 2025 revision,
in the same year ICMAP added an AI course. ERP (SAP/Oracle) skills are among the most
frequently demanded in Pakistani finance job adverts. **This is a curriculum-to-market gap
created by the 2025 reform, not merely inherited.**

## Claim 6 — "ICMAP response to students: 'We are not responsible for providing it' (practical training)"

**Verdict: Not found as a quotable source.** No published ICMAP statement to this effect was
located. Not repeated as fact.

**What IS confirmed (Tier A) and makes the grievance coherent:** ICMAP's "practical training"
is **coursework with an examination**, not employment. The PTMs carry a coaching/DLP fee
(PKR 15,500) and an exam fee (PKR 5,600) — students **pay** for practical training. ICAP's
equivalent is a **paid apprenticeship** at a regulated minimum stipend (PKR 27,500–93,500/month).

| | ICMAP PTM | ICAP training |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Coursework + exam | Employment |
| Who pays whom | **Student pays ICMAP ≈PKR 21,100/module** | **Employer pays student ≥PKR 27,500/month** |
| Employer placement | Not arranged | Authorised Training Organisation required |
| Duration | 6 modules | 30–42 months |

Across six modules a 12-year-route ICMAP student **pays ≈PKR 126,600 for "practical
training"**. An ICAP trainee **receives ≈PKR 1.3–1.6 million** over training. **That
≈PKR 1.5 million swing is the single largest economic difference between the two
qualifications and it is almost never stated.** See `../comparison/CMA_ACCA_CA_COMPARISON.md`.

## Claim 7 — "CMA now more expensive than CA yet offers lower stipends"

**Verdict: Confirmed by arithmetic (Tier A inputs) — and the gap is larger than claimed.**

**Direct fee comparison, 2026 official rates:**

| | ICMAP CMA (12-yr route) | ICAP CA (full-time route) |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | 13,200 | 22,000 |
| Per-paper cost | 23,900–41,000 (coaching + exam) | PRC 6,100 + exam 7,500; CAF 7,400 + exam ~4,600–7,000; CFAP 11,300 + exam ~5,700–14,400 |
| **Modelled body-fee total** | **≈727,000 (2025 rates) / ≈756,000 (2026)** | Materially lower per paper |
| **Stipend received** | **PKR 0** | **≈1.3–1.6 million over training** |

Two structural points that make the claim stronger than the way it is usually argued:

1. **ICMAP bundles mandatory coaching/DLP into the price.** A student must pay the
   coaching/DLP fee to become eligible to sit (Policy Guidelines §8) — it is not optional
   tuition. ICAP's per-paper fees do **not** include tuition; tuition is bought separately
   from a RAET, and ICAP runs means-tested schemes that cut it by **≥75%** for qualifying
   students. So ICMAP's headline fee is more inclusive, but it is also **unavoidable and
   un-discounted**.
2. **ICAP's fee structure rewards batching** (CAF: 7,000 for the first paper, 4,600 for each
   additional). **ICMAP's does not** — its per-course fee is flat. A student sitting 4 CAF
   papers pays ~5,200/paper; an ICMAP student sitting 3 SL-2 courses pays the full 9,000 exam
   fee on each.

**Net: the claim is correct, and the true gap is the fee difference plus ~PKR 1.5m of
foregone stipend.**

## Claim 8 — "90% of students are from middle / lower-middle class families"

**Verdict: Not found.** No ICMAP or third-party socio-economic profile of its student body
was located. The specific 90% figure has no traceable source.

**What can be said instead, from Tier A:** ICAP publishes a means test for its financial
support scheme set at **family income below PKR 100,000/month**, and offers ≥75% tuition
discounts to students meeting it. The existence and calibration of that threshold is
meaningful evidence about the income profile the profession expects — but it is ICAP's
evidence, not ICMAP's, and **no equivalent published ICMAP scheme was found at all** (see
Claim 10).

## Claim 9 — "Exam technique weakness / poor application to case studies is the main reason for failure"

**Verdict: Structurally supported (Tier A), source for "main reason" Not found.**

No ICMAP examiner's report quantifying failure causes was retrieved.

**Structurally supported** by ICMAP's own QPPS: in the one complete QPPS obtained, MCQs are
**20 of 100 marks**, with 50 marks in extended written work (report writing + presentation)
and 20 in short cases. A student who prepares via ICMAP's *Practice MCQs* portal is training
for 20% of the paper. The mark scheme itself predicts that application, not recall, decides
outcomes.

**Time allocation is the specific, trainable sub-skill:** the QPPS suggests 0.5 min/mark for
MCQs but 2.5 min/mark for report writing. Uniform pacing fails. See `EXAM_PATTERN.md` §2.2.

## Claim 10 — Financial support: the asymmetry nobody mentions

**Verdict: Confirmed by absence — and this is one of the most actionable findings in the CMA research.**

| | ICAP | ICMAP |
|---|---|---|
| Named means-tested scheme | **CAT–SFS**: ≥75% tuition discount, registration cut to Rs 2,000, PRC/CAF exam fee cut to Rs 1,000, family income threshold **< PKR 100,000/month**, ≥80% HSSC | **None found** |
| Merit endowment | **Students Endowment Fund**, with published attempt-based merit gates, needs assessment via **Ihsan Trust** | **None found** |
| Fee relief published on site | Yes, with eligibility criteria and numbers | **Not found** |

ICMAP's published fee page lists only **cost-increasing** or cost-neutral mechanisms:
instalments at a **+10% surcharge**, re-coaching at 50%, waivers at 50% of tuition. **No
needs-based reduction was found anywhere on ICMAP's public fee or student pages.**

⚠️ **Stated carefully:** this is an *absence of evidence* from public pages, not proof that
no scheme exists. ICMAP may operate unpublicised relief. But if a scheme is not discoverable
by a prospective student on the institute's own website, it does not function as a
recruitment or retention mechanism — which is the point.

**For Learms:** this is the highest-leverage content gap found for ICMAP students. If relief
exists but is undiscoverable, surfacing it is pure value. If it does not exist, students
deserve to know that before committing PKR 727,000.
