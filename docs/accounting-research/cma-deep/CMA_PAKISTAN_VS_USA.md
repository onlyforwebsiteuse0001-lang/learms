# CMA (ICMAP Pakistan) vs CMA (IMA USA) — Two Qualifications, One Name

**Area:** 2 (CMA deep dive — priority area)
**Researched:** 2026-09-30
**Why this file exists:** "CMA" denotes at least four different qualifications worldwide. The two
that matter to a Pakistani student — ICMAP's and IMA-USA's — are routinely conflated by coaching
centres, search engines and students. They are not variants of each other. They are structurally,
economically and temporally different products.

---

## 1. Head-to-head

| | **CMA — ICMAP (Pakistan)** | **CMA — IMA (USA)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Awarding body** | Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Pakistan | Institute of Management Accountants, Montvale NJ |
| **Full designation** | *Chartered* Management Accountant (renamed in Study Scheme-2025) | *Certified* Management Accountant |
| **Assessments** | **18 courses + 6 PTM** (12-yr entry) | **2 exam parts** |
| **Part/paper names** | O1–O6, M1–M6, S1–S6 + PTM1–6 | Part 1: Financial Planning, Performance and Analytics<br>Part 2: Strategic Financial Management |
| **Exam format** | CBE, 3 hours, 100 marks, MCQ + written + report + presentation | 4 hours: 100 MCQ + 2 essay scenarios |
| **Pass mark** | **Not published** for Scheme-2025 | **360 / 500** scaled |
| **Published pass rates** | **None** | ~40–45% globally *(Tier C — see §5)* |
| **Sittings per year** | 2 (6-month levels) | **3 windows**: Jan–Feb, May–Jun, Sep–Oct |
| **Sequencing** | Strictly sequential; max 6 courses/sitting; no level skipping | Either part in any order |
| **Time to qualify** | **4 years** (12-yr entry) / 2.5 / 2 | **3-year window** from entrance-fee payment; commonly 12–18 months |
| **Entry requirement** | **12 years of education** (intermediate) accepted | **Bachelor's degree** from an accredited institution, or a recognised professional certification |
| **Experience requirement** | 6 Practical Training Modules — **student pays ≈PKR 21,100 each** | **2 continuous years** in management accounting or financial management — **real paid employment**, completable before or within 7 years after passing |
| **Mandatory tuition** | **Yes** — coaching (≥60% attendance) or DLP | **No** — review courses are optional third-party purchases |
| **Body fees, first attempt** | **≈PKR 727,300** (12-yr route, 2025 rates) | **USD 1,585** professional / **≈USD 1,088–1,104** student |
| **Currency** | PKR | USD — **unhedged exposure for a Pakistani candidate** |
| **Recognition** | Pakistan; GCC; pathway MoUs (ACCA, CIMA, CPA Canada) | ~150 countries; strong in US multinationals and GCC |

## 2. The finding that should reframe the CMA conversation in Pakistan

**On body fees alone, the American CMA is cheaper than the Pakistani one.**

USD 1,585 at PKR 275–290/USD ≈ **PKR 436,000–460,000**.
ICMAP's 12-year-entry route ≈ **PKR 727,300**.

> ⚠️ **Exchange rate is an assumption, not a verified figure.** No Tier-A USD/PKR rate was captured
> in this session. Logged as `../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-011. The conclusion is robust across the
> plausible range: ICMAP's 12-year route exceeds the IMA professional total at any rate below
> ~PKR 459/USD, which is far outside any 2026 scenario. Treat the *direction* as reliable and the
> *magnitude* as indicative.

The same comparison in time: **2 exams versus 18 courses and 6 modules. 12–18 months versus 4 years.**

That a Pakistani professional body charges ~60% more and takes ~3× longer than the American body
using the same three letters is the most counter-intuitive result in this research, and it is
absent from every Pakistani comparison page reviewed.

### The qualifier that makes it fair

**The two are not open to the same person.** IMA requires a **bachelor's degree**. ICMAP admits
students at **12 years of education**. For the intermediate-qualified student — ICMAP's largest
and most economically constrained intake — **the IMA CMA is simply not available.** ICMAP's
12-year entry route is not competing with IMA; it is serving a population IMA excludes.

The comparison bites hardest for the **16-year-degree entrant**, who *can* choose:

| 16-year graduate's options | Cost | Duration | Portability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICMAP CMA (ML-2 entry, 9 courses + 3 PTM) | ≈PKR 463,900 | 2 years | Pakistan/GCC |
| IMA CMA (professional member) | ≈PKR 436,000–460,000 | 12–18 months | ~150 countries |

**At parity of cost, the IMA route is faster and travels further.** ICMAP's counter-arguments are
real but need stating rather than assuming: local statutory and regulatory grounding, Pakistani
tax and corporate law content, domestic employer recognition, PKR-denominated payment with no
currency risk, and a membership network inside Pakistan. None of those are trivial. But they are
*arguments*, and students are currently not being given the comparison in which to weigh them.

**Recommendation for Learms:** present this comparison to 16-year-entry users explicitly. It is
the highest-stakes uninformed decision identified in this research.

## 3. What each one is actually good at

### ICMAP CMA is the better instrument when…
- The student has **12 or 14 years of education** — IMA is closed to them.
- The work is **Pakistan-specific**: Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sales Tax Act 1990, Companies Act
  2017, SECP filings. IMA's syllabus contains none of this.
- The employer is a Pakistani firm that recognises the designation and not the American one.
- The student needs **PKR-denominated, instalment-friendly payment** rather than a USD card
  transaction — a real constraint given Pakistani foreign-exchange and card limitations.

### IMA CMA is the better instrument when…
- The student **already holds a bachelor's degree** and is time-constrained.
- The employer is a **multinational** or the target market is the **GCC**, where IMA recognition
  is strong.
- The student has **real paid management-accounting employment** — IMA's experience requirement is
  satisfied by a job, whereas ICMAP's is satisfied by *paying for training modules*.

### The experience requirement is the sharpest philosophical difference
IMA says: *go and do the work for two years, and we will certify that you did.*
ICMAP says: *pay us ≈PKR 126,600 for six modules, and we will certify that you are trained.*

One externalises verification to the labour market; the other internalises it as a revenue line.
This is the same structural criticism made of ICMAP's PTMs in
`STUDENT_PROBLEMS.md` and it is reinforced by the contrast with **ICAP, which pays its trainees
PKR 27,500–93,500 per month.** Among the three routes available to a Pakistani management-
accounting student, ICMAP is the only one where practical training is a **net cash outflow**.

## 4. Naming collisions — the full set

| Designation | Body | Country | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMA | **ICMAP** | Pakistan | The subject of this research |
| CMA | **IMA** | USA | Dominates English-language search results |
| CMA | **ICMAI** | India | Dominates *fee-related* search results; ₹ pricing, Foundation/Intermediate/Final structure |
| CMA | **ICMAB** | Bangladesh | Admission ≈BDT 22,500, ≈BDT 5,500/level — an order of magnitude below ICMAP |
| ACMA/FCMA | **CIMA** | UK | Also "Chartered Management Accountant" — **direct collision with ICMAP's new 2025 branding** |

**Two operational warnings for Agent 1:**

1. **Search for "CMA fees" and you get India.** Every such query in this research was polluted by
   ICMAI content. Any Learms content-ingestion or search feature must disambiguate by body, not
   by acronym. Queries must be constrained with "ICMAP" or "Pakistan".
2. **ICMAP renamed its designation into a collision.** By adopting "*Chartered* Management
   Accountant" in Study Scheme-2025, ICMAP now shares its designation wording with CIMA's
   ACMA/FCMA. Given that ICMAP simultaneously operates a CIMA pathway (LLG ACMA PKR 63,500 /
   FCMA PKR 53,200), this is a live source of student confusion inside ICMAP's own product line.

## 5. Source quality — read this before using the numbers

**ICMAP figures are Tier A** (the institute's own circulars and fee pages).

**IMA figures are Tier C.** No IMA page was fetched directly in this session; the figures are
triangulated across five independent secondary sources. Where they agree, confidence is
reasonable. Where they disagree, the disagreement is reported rather than averaged.

| Item | Agreement | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Entrance fee, professional | $300 — **5/5 sources** | High confidence |
| IMA professional membership | $295/yr — **4/5**; one outlier at $500 | High confidence; outlier rejected |
| Exam fee per part, professional | $495 — **4/5**; outliers $545 and $643.10 | Moderate–high; **$495 adopted** |
| First-year professional total | $1,585 — **3/5** | Moderate–high |
| Entrance fee, student | $225 — **4/5** | High |
| **Exam fee per part, student** | **$370 / $407 / $415 / $480.26 — no agreement** | **Rejected.** Range only: $370–480 |
| Pass mark | 360/500 | Consistent with long-standing IMA practice |
| Pass rate | "~40–45% globally" — **1 source, unattributed** | **Tier C, weak.** Do not present as fact |

**Two deliberate refusals:**
- The **student exam fee is not asserted**. Four sources give four numbers spanning 30%. Logged
  as a contradiction rather than resolved by picking one.
- The **~40–45% pass rate is not treated as comparable** to ACCA's published per-paper rates. It
  is one unattributed secondary claim. Using it to rank IMA against ACCA would be exactly the
  error criticised in `../comparison/CMA_ACCA_CA_COMPARISON.md` §6.

**Follow-up required:** fetch `https://www.imanet.org/` fee and membership pages directly to raise
these to Tier A. Not done in this session — recorded honestly rather than papered over.
