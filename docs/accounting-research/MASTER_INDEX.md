## The five findings that matter most

1. **The ≈PKR 1.5–2.0 million practical-training swing.** ICAP pays trainees PKR 27,500–93,500/month; ICMAP students pay ≈PKR 126,600 for their training modules. This dominates every other economic factor and appears on no Pakistani comparison page.
2. **Local content is the top-ranked student demand.** Two peer-reviewed studies, 48 interviews, three actor groups, one prescription.
3. **A constitutional deadline of 1 Jan 2028 to eliminate riba** — and neither Pakistani body core-examines Islamic finance in its 2025 scheme.
4. **ACCA's difficulty cliff is at Applied Skills, not Strategic Professional** (BT 87% → PM 40%). ICMAP publishes no pass rates at all; the transparency asymmetry is itself a finding.
5. **The brief is wrong in at least fourteen places** — see `CONTRADICTIONS.md`. Most consequentially: the CA structure is obsolete, the CMA course count is half the real figure, and the CMA salary figure is unsupportable.

## Source tiering used throughout

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Official body document or page (ICMAP, ICAP, ACCA, IMA, SECP, FBR) |
| **B** | Peer-reviewed research, or semi-official |
| **C** | Credible secondary (established publisher, triangulated) |
| **D** | Anecdotal, grievance, or marketing content — **proves a grievance is held, never that a fact is true** (D-007) |
| **E** | Agent 7's own model, built on Tier-A inputs and labelled as modelled |

**No finding in this research is stated without a tier and a source. Where a source could not be
found, "not found" is written instead.**
