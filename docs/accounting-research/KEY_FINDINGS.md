## ICMAP CMA — Study Scheme-2025 (priority area)

1. Study Scheme-2025 took effect **16 July 2025** (Circular ICMAP/HO/Edu/106/2025, 15 Jul 2025). **[A]**
2. Structure is three levels of six courses — Operational (O1–O6), Managerial (M1–M6), Strategic (S1–S6) — plus six Practical Training Modules. **[A]**
3. Each level runs **6 months**. **[A]**
4. ★ A 12-year-education entrant takes **18 courses + 6 PTM**, not the "09 courses" the brief states. **[A]** (C-001)
5. 14-year entry: **12 courses + 3 PTM**, minimum 2.5 years. **[A]**
6. 16-year entry: **9 courses + 3 PTM**, minimum 2 years. **[A]**
7. Minimum duration for 12-year entry is **4 years**. **[A]**
8. Exams are **CBE**, 3 hours, 100 marks. **[A]**
9. Maximum **6 courses per sitting**, strictly sequential; no level skipping. **[A]**
10. The intermediate award is **Associate Financial Accountant (AFA)** — OL + ML + PTM-L1, ~2.5 years. **[A]**
11. ICMAP renamed its designation to ***Chartered* Management Accountant** in Scheme-2025. **[A]**
12. ★ **M2 is Business Taxation** and **S2 is Advance Taxation** — the brief swaps them. **[A]** (C-002, C-003)
13. Coaching route requires **≥60% attendance plus a presentation**; DLP requires the presentation only. **[A]**
14. Tuition is **mandatory and bundled** into the fee — unlike ACCA and ICAP. **[A]**
15. Legacy students get **6 attempts at normal fee, then double fee**. **[A]**
16. ⚠ **No pass mark is published** for Scheme-2025. Do not assert 50%. **[A-absence]** (B-007)
17. ⚠ **No attempt cap and no time limit** are published for Scheme-2025. **[A-absence]**
18. ⚠ **ICMAP publishes no pass rates at all** — for any subject, any session, ever. **[A-absence]** (B-003)
19. ⚠ **No completion or attrition statistics** are published. **[A-absence]**
20. Full QPPS for all 18 Scheme-2025 subjects **could not be located**. **[A-absence]** (B-006)
21. The PM6 QPPS (Scheme-2018/2023 vintage) allocates **MCQ 20 / short cases 40 / report writing 30 / presentation 30**. **[A]**
22. **75 of 180 minutes** in PM6 go to a single report. Time, not knowledge, is the binding constraint. **[A]**
23. Model-paper MCQ stems are anchored to **named standards** — IAS 19, IAS 20, IFRS 2, IFRS 9, ISA 200, Companies Act 2017 s.87. **[A]**
24. **Python and ERP were dropped** from practical module titles between Scheme-2018/2023 and Scheme-2025. **[A]**
25. Only two genuinely new courses exist: **O2 Gen. AI & Business Productivity** and **M4 Digital Accounting & Financial Modelling**. **[A]**
26. ICMAP published **"Interim Study Resources for New Subjects"** — an official admission that permanent material for O2/M4 was not ready at launch. **[A]**
27. **PTM1 is Data Analytics & Visualisation (Power BI)** — named tool, examinable. **[A]**
28. **S1 adds Sustainability Reporting** to Advanced Financial Accounting. **[A]**
29. Exams run in **9 Pakistani cities**, implying 8–12 unmodelled travel trips over a full route. **[A]**
30. Remote-access exams cost **1.5× the normal fee**. **[A]**
31. Overseas candidates pay **USD 100 per course**. **[A]**
32. ⚠ No academic study of ICMAP students specifically exists. **Not one was found.** **[A-absence]**

## CMA (ICMAP) vs CMA (IMA USA)

97. IMA: professional membership $295/yr, entrance $300, **$495 per part** ⇒ **$1,585 first year**. **[C, 4–5 source agreement]**
98. ★★ **On body fees the American CMA is cheaper than the Pakistani one** (≈PKR 436,000–460,000 vs 727,300) and ~3× faster. **[E on C/A]**
99. **But IMA requires a bachelor's degree** — it is closed to ICMAP's 12- and 14-year entrants. The two serve different populations. **[C]**
100. ★ For a **16-year graduate** the routes cost about the same; the IMA route is faster and far more portable. This fork is almost never presented. **[E]**
101. IMA experience = **2 years of ordinary paid employment**; ICMAP = **6 modules the student pays for**. **[C/A]**
102. ⚠ IMA **student** exam fee could not be resolved — four sources give $370 / $407 / $415 / $480.26. **Not asserted.** **[C]** (C-013)
103. ⚠ The "~40–45% IMA pass rate" rests on **one unattributed secondary claim** and is not comparable to ACCA's published rates. **[C]**
104. **"CMA" denotes at least four qualifications** — ICMAP, IMA, ICMAI (India), ICMAB (Bangladesh). **[A/C]**
105. ICMAB Bangladesh charges ≈BDT 22,500 admission and ≈BDT 5,500/level — **an order of magnitude below ICMAP**. **[C]**
