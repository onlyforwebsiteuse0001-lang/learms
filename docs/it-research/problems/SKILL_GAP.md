## Topic: Software engineering skill is broader than coding
### Source: IEEE Computer Society, 2024
### Key Finding: SWEBOK v4.0 includes requirements, architecture, design, construction, testing, operations, maintenance, configuration management, management, process, models/methods, quality, security, professional practice, economics, and foundations.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms portfolios should assess testing, collaboration, maintainability, security, and deployment, not only syntax.
### Citation: https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering

## Topic: Modern delivery skill includes flow and reliability metrics
### Source: DORA/Google Cloud, 2022
### Key Finding: DORA uses delivery and operational metrics such as deployment frequency, lead time, time to restore, change failure rate, and reliability.
### Relevance to Learms: Job-ready tracks should include Git, CI/CD, deployment, monitoring, and incident-thinking basics.
### Citation: https://dora.dev/research/2022/dora-report/2022-dora-accelerate-state-of-devops-report.pdf

## Topic: Pakistan skills demand needs direct industry evidence
### Source: P@SHA, 2025; PSEB, 2024
### Key Finding: P@SHA’s Skills Survey 2025 is designed to identify critical skills gaps and training priorities from Pakistan IT/ITeS companies; PSEB 2024 frames HR skills/capacity building as a pillar for industry growth.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should prioritize Pakistan employer-backed skill gaps after deeper extraction from P@SHA/PSEB documents.
