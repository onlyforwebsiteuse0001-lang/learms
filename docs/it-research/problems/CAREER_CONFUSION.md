## Topic: Computing has multiple distinct disciplines that students often blur together
### Source: ACM/IEEE/AIS curriculum reports, 2017-2023
### Key Finding: Formal computing curricula distinguish Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Information Systems, Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity, and Data Science, each with different competencies and outcomes.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should diagnose learner goals before recommending a path; “IT” cannot mean everything in the UI.
### Citation: https://csed.acm.org/ ; https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/it2017.pdf ; https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/is2020.pdf ; https://dstf.acm.org/

## Topic: Global language rankings can increase confusion if shown without context
### Source: Stack Overflow, 2024; GitHub, 2024; JetBrains, 2024
### Key Finding: Different surveys rank languages differently depending on survey method, repository activity, or ecosystem measures.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should show “best for your goal” rather than a single universal ranking.
### Citation: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/ ; https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-2024/ ; https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/devecosystem-2024/

## Topic: Pakistan career recommendations need current local data
### Source: P@SHA, 2025; PSEB, 2024
### Key Finding: P@SHA and PSEB provide Pakistan IT/ITeS skills and industry context, but role-level career advice requires deeper extraction and current job-posting validation.
### Relevance to Learms: Show confidence levels on Pakistan demand claims.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Skills-Survey-2025.pdf ; https://www.techdestination.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/PSEB-ANNUAL-REPORT-7_compressed.pdf

## Career Confusion Patterns

| Student Thought | Hidden Ambiguity | Learms Response |
|---|---|---|
| “I want to do IT.” | CS vs SE vs IT ops vs freelancing vs cyber | Goal quiz with examples of daily work |
| “AI has scope.” | ML researcher vs data analyst vs AI app builder vs prompt/tool user | Show prerequisite ladder and job realities |
| “Which language is best?” | goal-dependent | language recommender by goal/market |
| “Frontend is saturated.” | no local data yet | show confidence and evidence freshness |
| “Cybersecurity means hacking.” | offensive vs defensive vs GRC vs AppSec | ethical/legal path split |
| “Full-stack is enough.” | may lack testing/deployment/security | job-ready checklist |

## UI/UX Recommendation

- Career cards should show daily tasks, prerequisites, first project, Pakistan evidence confidence, salary evidence freshness, and “not for you if...” warnings.
- Hide advanced/specialized paths until foundations are visible to reduce choice overload.

## Gaps

- Need salary distribution by experience and city.
