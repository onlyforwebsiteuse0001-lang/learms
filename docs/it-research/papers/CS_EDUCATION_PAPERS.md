# CS Education Papers — Agent 5

- Status: Draft v0.1, Area 11 in progress

## Topic: First-year students struggle with independent programming
### Source: McCracken et al., 2001
### Key Finding: A multi-national, multi-institutional study found first-year CS students struggled on programming assessments requiring program construction.
### Relevance to Learms: Early programming tracks must include small independent construction tasks, not only syntax recall.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/572133.572137

## Topic: Novice programming research covers knowledge, strategies, and mental models
### Source: Robins, Rountree, and Rountree, 2003
### Key Finding: The review describes challenges in learning and teaching programming, including novice/expert differences.
### Relevance to Learms: Build explicit mental-model teaching and progression from tracing to generation.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1076/csed.13.2.137.14200

## Topic: CS1 has extensive research but persistent problems
### Source: Luxton-Reilly et al., 2018
### Key Finding: A systematic literature review maps introductory programming research and recurring learner challenges.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should maintain a CS1 evidence map and not assume a single silver bullet.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/3293881.3295779

## Topic: Logic and misconception errors are common
### Source: Spohrer & Soloway, 1986; Brown & Altadmri, 2018
### Key Finding: Classic and modern studies show novice programming errors/misconceptions persist, including logic errors that may not be caught by syntax diagnostics.
### Relevance to Learms: Hidden tests, tracing, and misconception-specific feedback are necessary.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/6138.6145 ; https://doi.org/10.1145/3160489.3160493

## Topic: Peer instruction and flipped classroom are relevant CS teaching methods
### Source: Porter et al., 2016; Giannakos et al., 2014
### Key Finding: Peer instruction has multi-institutional CS evidence; flipped classroom research has been reviewed for CS education.
### Relevance to Learms: Concepts should support discussion prompts, pre-work, and active in-app checks.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/2839509.2844642 ; https://doi.org/10.1145/2691352.2691354
