## Topic: Career guidance should reduce path overload
### Source: Iyengar and Lepper, 2000; ACM/IEEE curriculum sources
### Key Finding: Choice overload research suggests too many options can reduce action in some settings; computing curricula show many legitimate pathways.
### Relevance to Learms: Career UI should group paths, diagnose goals, and progressively reveal options.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.79.6.995 ; https://csed.acm.org/

## Topic: Career confidence needs self-efficacy support
### Source: Beaubouef and Mason, 2005; Rosenstein et al., 2020
### Key Finding: Attrition and impostor phenomenon are concerns in CS education.
### Relevance to Learms: Career guidance should show attainable milestones and normalize switching paths.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/1083431.1083474 ; https://doi.org/10.1145/3328778.3366815
