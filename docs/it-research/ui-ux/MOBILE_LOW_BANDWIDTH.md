## Learms Mobile UX Warnings

- Coding on mobile is possible for short exercises, but full projects may require laptop/desktop access.
- Do not overpromise “become full-stack from phone only” without evidence.
- Offer mobile-friendly concept practice and desktop-friendly project tasks.
