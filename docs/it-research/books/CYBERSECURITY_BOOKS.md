## Learms Ethical Guardrails

1. Start with legal/ethical warning.
2. Use isolated labs only.
3. Emphasize defensive explanation and remediation.
4. Map hacking exercises to OWASP/NICE competencies.
5. Avoid harmful operational guidance outside lab context.
