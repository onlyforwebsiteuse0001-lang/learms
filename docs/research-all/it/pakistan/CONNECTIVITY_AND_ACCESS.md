## Topic: Mobile broadband coverage is high but usage gaps remain
### Source: GSMA, 2024
### Key Finding: GSMA reported that 81% of Pakistan's adult population was covered by 3G/4G, smartphone ownership reached 63% by end-2023, but only 23% of the population subscribed to mobile internet services.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should design for mobile-aware, low-data, trust-building access rather than assuming coverage equals usage.
### Citation: https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/gsma-highlights-pakistans-digital-progress-and-potential-to-spur-accelerated-economic-growth/

## Topic: PTA reports strong broadband growth
### Source: PTA, 2024
### Key Finding: PTA stated cellular mobile services reached 91% and 4G services 81% of population, with 196M telecom subscribers, 142.3M broadband subscribers, and 58.4% broadband penetration by 2024 reporting.
### Relevance to Learms: Pakistan learning UX should be web/mobile responsive, but also resilient to uneven quality and affordability barriers.
### Citation: https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/chairman-pta-presents-annual-report-2024-to-chairman-of-the-senate-of-pakistan-1169784892-2024-12-18

## Learms Access Design Implications

| Evidence | Product Implication |
|---|---|
| High 3G/4G coverage but lower usage | do not equate infrastructure with learner access |
| Smartphone ownership but low mobile internet subscription | compress assets; optional video; text-first |
| Broadband growth | web app viable for many learners |
| Usage gap | offline/download mode and low-data mode important |

## Low-Data Content Policy

1. Text and code first.
2. Video optional, downloadable, and transcript-backed.
3. Diagrams compressed and with alt text.
4. Code exercises should save state and resume.
5. Avoid mandatory heavy IDE downloads for beginner tracks.
