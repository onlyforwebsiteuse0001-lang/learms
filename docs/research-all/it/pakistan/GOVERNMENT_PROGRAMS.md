## Topic: DigiSkills is a major public digital-skills program
### Source: DigiSkills.pk, 2026
### Key Finding: DigiSkills describes itself as Pakistan's largest training program, offering free online courses with certificates; site claims 5.5M+ trainings since 2018.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should not duplicate basic freelancing/WordPress/digital-skills training blindly; it should complement with deeper practice, diagnostics, and portfolio review.
### Citation: https://digiskills.pk/

## Topic: DigiSkills course list covers freelancing and digital-work categories
### Source: DigiSkills.pk, 2026
### Key Finding: Official “What is DigiSkills.pk” page lists courses including freelancing, affiliate marketing, data analytics/BI, e-commerce management, digital marketing, digital literacy, WordPress, graphic design, SEO, and more.
### Relevance to Learms: These courses indicate practical market interest areas for Pakistani learners.
### Citation: https://www.digiskills.pk/WhatsDigiSkills.aspx

## Topic: Pakistan's AI policy draft emphasizes capacity building and infrastructure
### Source: MoITT, 2023
### Key Finding: The National AI Policy draft includes capacity building, AI education, National AI Fund, Centres of Excellence, data standards, compute infrastructure, datasets, and AI governance.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms AI/data tracks should align with national AI upskilling, ethics, governance, and practical project needs.
### Citation: https://moitt.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/National%20AI%20Policy.pdf
