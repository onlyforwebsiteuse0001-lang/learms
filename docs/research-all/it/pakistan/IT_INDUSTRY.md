## Topic: Pakistan IT exports and workforce are strategic national priorities
### Source: PSEB Annual Report, 2024
### Key Finding: PSEB frames HR skills/capacity building, infrastructure development, ease of doing business, and global outreach as key pillars; report text states IT exports reached USD 1.53 billion in the first five months of FY 2024-25 and cites over 600,000 IT/ITeS professionals.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should align learning paths to export-oriented roles, practical portfolio work, and globally sellable services.
### Citation: https://www.techdestination.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/PSEB-ANNUAL-REPORT-7_compressed.pdf

## Topic: Pakistan industry skills need employer-backed validation
### Source: P@SHA Skills Survey, 2025
### Key Finding: P@SHA's skills survey is designed to identify critical skill gaps and IT/ITeS training priorities using industry input.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should convert P@SHA role/skill findings into prioritized local learning tracks.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Skills-Survey-2025.pdf

## Topic: Public salary evidence exists but is stale
### Source: P@SHA Salary Survey, 2021
### Key Finding: P@SHA Salary Survey 2021 gives historical role/language salary signals but is too old for current salary promises.
### Relevance to Learms: Salary cards must show evidence year and confidence level.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Salary-Survey-2021-1.pdf
