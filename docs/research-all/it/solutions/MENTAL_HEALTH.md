## Topic: Impostor phenomenon is relevant to CS students
### Source: Rosenstein, Raghu, and Porter, 2020; Chen et al., 2024
### Key Finding: Research identifies impostor phenomenon among CS students; Chen et al. report associations between impostor syndrome and code comprehension behavior/outcomes in an exploratory study.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should avoid shame-driven rankings and should include progress evidence and normalization of struggle.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/3328778.3366815 ; https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-61569-6_2

## Topic: Attrition requires supportive interventions
### Source: Beaubouef and Mason, 2005
### Key Finding: High attrition in CS is a recognized problem requiring strategy and support.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should provide early wins, mentorship, and clear recovery after failure.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1145/1083431.1083474

## Topic: Effective learning reduces panic-cramming
### Source: Dunlosky et al., 2013
### Key Finding: Distributed practice and practice testing are more effective than many low-utility study techniques.
### Relevance to Learms: Spaced plans can reduce last-minute overload and build durable confidence.
### Citation: https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266
