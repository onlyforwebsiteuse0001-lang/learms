## Topic: Pakistan IT growth makes skills alignment urgent
### Source: PSEB, 2024
### Key Finding: PSEB frames HR skills and capacity building as a key pillar for Pakistan IT industry growth and reports export/workforce context in its annual report.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should align paths with Pakistan export, freelancing, and IT services needs.
### Citation: https://www.techdestination.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/PSEB-ANNUAL-REPORT-7_compressed.pdf

## Topic: Pakistan skill gaps need employer-backed data
### Source: P@SHA, 2025
### Key Finding: P@SHA Skills Survey 2025 is designed to identify critical skill gaps and training priorities through a national survey of IT/ITeS companies.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should extract P@SHA skills categories and map them to modules.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Skills-Survey-2025.pdf

## Topic: Public salary evidence exists but can be stale
### Source: P@SHA, 2021
### Key Finding: Public P@SHA Salary Survey 2021 includes role/language salary categories, but it is old for current salary decisions.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should label salary data by year and confidence.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Salary-Survey-2021-1.pdf

## Pakistan Learning Solution Ideas

| Certification cost | ROI calculator for AWS/Azure/CompTIA/etc. | Needs certification price + salary data |
