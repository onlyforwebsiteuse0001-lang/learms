## Topic: BLS software developer and QA data gives U.S. occupational benchmark
### Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025
### Key Finding: BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook provides U.S.-specific pay, education, number of jobs, and outlook for software developers, QA analysts, and testers.
### Relevance to Learms: Useful for global context and role descriptions, but not directly transferable to Pakistan.
### Citation: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm

## Topic: Programmer job category can differ from modern developer roles
### Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025
### Key Finding: BLS OOH reports computer programmers separately and indicates a negative outlook for that U.S. occupation category.
### Relevance to Learms: Avoid teaching “programming only” as career path; emphasize product, testing, design, and systems skills.
### Citation: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-programmers.htm

## Topic: Stack Overflow survey offers developer-sourced global signals
### Source: Stack Overflow Developer Survey, 2024
### Key Finding: Stack Overflow reports responses from over 65,000 developers across 185 countries, including salary, technology usage, and AI-tool sentiment.
### Relevance to Learms: Useful for technology popularity and global salary context with survey-method caveats.
### Citation: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/

## Benchmark Interpretation Rules

| Source Type | Good For | Not Good For |
|---|---|---|
| BLS | U.S. standardized occupation/pay/outlook | Pakistan salaries |
| Stack Overflow | global developer trends and self-reported salaries | representative labor-market truth |
| GitHub | repository activity | salaries/jobs |
| JetBrains | ecosystem preferences | country-specific salary guarantees |
| Levels.fyi | high-end tech compensation | general market |

## Learms Product Recommendation

- Use global salary data only behind a “global context” label.
- For Pakistani learners, show local market and remote-market separately.
- Explain compensation components: base salary, bonus, equity, freelancing revenue, and benefits.
