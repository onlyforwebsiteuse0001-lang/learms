## Topic: Freelancing is central in Pakistan digital-skills messaging
### Source: DigiSkills.pk, 2026
### Key Finding: DigiSkills positions its free courses around freelancing, online job marketplaces, digital skills, and certificates, with courses such as freelancing, WordPress, digital marketing, SEO, e-commerce, and data analytics/BI.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms should support freelance-readiness skills: portfolio, communication, scope, pricing, revision handling, and quality.
### Citation: https://digiskills.pk/ ; https://www.digiskills.pk/WhatsDigiSkills.aspx

## Topic: PSEB global outreach/export focus matters for remote services
### Source: PSEB Annual Report, 2024
### Key Finding: PSEB emphasizes global outreach and export growth for IT/ITeS, with focus on key markets and HR skills/capacity.
### Relevance to Learms: Learms can prepare learners for export-style service delivery, not only local jobs.

## Freelance Readiness Rubric

| Skill | Beginner Evidence | Portfolio Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Communication | writes clear proposal | client-style project brief |
| Scoping | lists deliverables | scope document and exclusions |
| Pricing | estimates effort | sample quote with assumptions |
| Quality | tests/checklist | before-after review |
| Delivery | deploys/exports work | live link/repository/case study |
| Support | handles revisions | revision policy |
| Ethics | avoids fake claims | transparent skill profile |
