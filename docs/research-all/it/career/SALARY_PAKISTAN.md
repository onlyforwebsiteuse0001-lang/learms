## Topic: P@SHA salary data is employer-backed but old
### Source: P@SHA Salary Survey, 2021
### Key Finding: P@SHA Salary Survey 2021 reports salary benchmarks across Pakistan IT/ITeS companies, employees, roles, levels, and technologies; public excerpted search results show programmer averages by stack and level.
### Relevance to Learms: Useful for historical Pakistan salary baseline, but must be labeled old and not used as current promise.
### Citation: https://www.pasha.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Salary-Survey-2021-1.pdf

## Topic: PayScale Pakistan software developer salary is current-ish but sample-limited/private
### Source: PayScale, 2026
### Key Finding: PayScale reports an average software developer salary in Pakistan around PKR 826,592/year based on salary profiles, with wide ranges and last-updated metadata.
### Relevance to Learms: Useful as a triangulation source only; private self-reported samples may not represent the full market.
### Citation: https://www.payscale.com/research/PK/Job=Software_Developer/Salary

## Salary Evidence Confidence

| Source | Strength | Weakness | Learms Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| P@SHA Salary Survey 2021 | industry association, company/employee sample | stale for 2026 | historical baseline |
| PayScale Pakistan 2026 | current web salary aggregation | small/unknown sample and methodology | rough triangulation |
| Job posts | current openings | many omit salary | demand/skill extraction |
| Recruiter interviews | practical insight | anecdotal | qualitative notes |
| Company salary bands | high confidence | hard to obtain | gold standard |

## Draft Pakistan Salary Policy for Learms

- Never show a single salary as guaranteed.
- Show ranges by source, experience, city, and date.
- Mark confidence: high/medium/low.
- Separate local employment, remote work, and freelancing income.
- Add cost/ROI for certifications only with price and hiring evidence.
