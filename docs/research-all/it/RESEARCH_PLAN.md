# Agent 5 — IT Deep Research Plan

## Operating Rules

- Research-only outputs: `.md`, `.json`, `.csv`, `.yaml`.
- Every substantive finding must cite a source with author/organization, year, title, and URL/DOI where available.
- Mark unsupported or unavailable claims as `not found` rather than inventing data.
- Prioritize depth, contradictions, gaps, and Learms relevance over unsourced breadth.
