## 2. Question Paper Pattern and Structure (QPPS)

ICMAP governs each paper's composition through a per-subject **QPPS** document, published at
`https://www.icmap.com.pk/cma_pattern_structure_question_paper/<CODE>.pdf`.

Every model paper carries this standing note:

> "The questions made available through this Model Paper reflects the pattern of questions to
> be tested in exams. However, their numbers will vary as per the **Question Paper Pattern and
> Structure (QPPS)** and respective weightage of the subject."

### 2.1 A worked QPPS example (Tier A) — PM6 Communication Skills

This is the one complete QPPS structure retrieved in full. **It is from Study Scheme 2018
(updated 2023), effective February 2024 examinations** — i.e. the predecessor scheme.

| Section | Content | Composition | Marks | Suggested duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Multiple Choice Questions | 10 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks | **20** | 10 minutes |
| B | Short Cases | 2 × 20 marks | **20** | 20 minutes |
| C | Report Writing | 1 × 30 marks | **30** | 1 hour 15 minutes |
| D | Presentation / Topic | | **30** | 15 minutes |
| | **Total** | | **100** | |

### 2.2 What this tells us structurally

1. **MCQs are a minority of marks** — 20% in this example. The dominant weight sits in short
   cases and extended written work. Any "practice MCQs and you'll pass" strategy is
   mis-calibrated to the mark scheme. ICMAP's own *Practice MCQs* portal is therefore
   necessary but far from sufficient.
2. **Time per mark is deliberately uneven.** Section A allows 10 minutes for 20 marks
   (0.5 min/mark); Section C allows 75 minutes for 30 marks (2.5 min/mark). Students who pace
   uniformly will run out of time. **Time allocation per section is itself an examinable skill
   and a trainable one** — a concrete, high-value Learms feature.
3. **Both MCQ weightings exist in one section** (1-mark and 2-mark MCQs). Practice tooling
   that treats all MCQs as equal-weight will mis-estimate readiness.

### 2.3 Observed question style from CBE model papers

From ICMAP's published model papers across ML2/SL1/SL2 subjects, the MCQ stems are
predominantly **standard-recognition and definition items** anchored to named standards and
statutes, e.g.:

- *"As per IAS 19, Employee Benefits, which one of the following is not a category of employee benefits?"*
- *"Government grants related to assets, including non-monetary grants at fair value, shall be presented in ______."* (IAS 20)
- *"____ is an attitude that includes a questioning mind, being alert to conditions which may indicate possible misstatement due to error or fraud…"* (professional scepticism, ISA)
- *"As restricted under section 87 of the Companies Act, 2017, no company shall … hold any shares in its ______"*
- *"The discount rate that makes NPV of all cash flows from a particular project equal to zero is classified as ______"* (IRR)
- *"IFRS 2 applies to share-based payment transactions in which an entity ______ goods or services"*
- *"IFRS 9 Financial Instruments specifies how an entity should classify and measure financial assets…"*

**Finding for Agent 1 and Agent 2:** ICMAP MCQs are overwhelmingly **anchored to a named
authority** — a specific IAS/IFRS, a specific section of the Companies Act 2017, a specific
ISA. This is excellent news for an evidence-grounded knowledge graph: every such question maps
cleanly to a citable standard, and the `Concept.evidence` field can hold the standard
reference itself. It also means **spaced repetition over standard-to-rule mappings is
directly aligned with how ICMAP actually tests** — see `../solutions/STUDY_TECHNIQUES.md`.

## 3. Examination centres

Examinations are conducted in **nine cities**: Faisalabad, Hyderabad, Karachi, Lahore,
Islamabad, Multan, Peshawar, Sukkur, Quetta.

Source: ICMAP June-2025 Examination Circular, Ref. JUN-2025-OL-SL-PCTME-LLG/01/05/25, 14 May
2025 — https://www.icmap.com.pk/ExamNotice_PDF/circular_exam_ol1tosl2_pctme_llg_June2025.pdf

**Implication:** a student in, say, Gilgit, Gwadar, Bahawalpur or Abbottabad must travel to
sit every paper. Over 18 courses plus 6 PTMs, at up to 6 papers per session, that is on the
order of **8–12 separate examination trips** across the qualification. Travel and
accommodation are a real and completely unmodelled cost — none of the cost estimates in
`STUDY_SCHEME_2025_COMPLETE.md` §12 include them, and neither does any source found.
The remote-access option exists but costs **1.5× the exam fee**.

## 4. Eligibility to sit (Policy Guidelines §8)

| Route | Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Coaching classes** | ≥ **60% lecture attendance**; appeared in the **mandatory presentation**; all dues cleared; exam fee paid |
| **Distance Learning Program (DLP)** | Appeared in the **mandatory presentation**; all dues cleared; exam fee paid |

Enrolment must be completed **at least one month before examination forms are submitted**.

Note the **mandatory presentation requirement applies to both routes** — including DLP. This
is an oral/presentation assessment gate on exam entry that is rarely mentioned in study
guidance.

## 5. Attempt limits — what is and is not stated

**What the Study Scheme-2025 policy circular states:**
- Legacy (Study Scheme-2018) students who had completed eligibility or already attempted a
  course may continue at the **normal examination fee for up to six (6) attempts**. After six
  attempts they pay **double the examination fee** then applicable.

**What it does NOT state:**
- ❌ Any pass mark / passing percentage.
- ❌ Any maximum-attempts limit for Study Scheme-2025 students.
- ❌ Any overall time limit to complete the qualification.

**Agent 7 does not assert a 50% pass mark for ICMAP**, despite that being the commonly
repeated figure, because no Tier-A source for Study Scheme-2025 was located. Recorded in
`../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-007. Contrast: ACCA states a fixed 50% pass mark publicly and
unambiguously.

## 6. Pass rates: not published

ICMAP publishes result gazettes (lists of successful candidates) but **not denominators**, so
pass rates cannot be derived from public data. See `../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-003.

This matters for Agent 1: **ICMAP difficulty cannot be empirically seeded the way ACCA's can**
(ACCA publishes per-paper pass rates every session). For ICMAP, `Concept.difficulty` must be
seeded from level position and expert judgement, and the `confidence` field on those seeds
should be correspondingly lower. Recommended seeds are in
[`../data/icmap_cma_scheme_2025.json`](../data/icmap_cma_scheme_2025.json).
