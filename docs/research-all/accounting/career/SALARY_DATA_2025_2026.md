# Accounting Salaries in Pakistan, 2025–2026 — Triangulated, with Confidence Levels

**Area:** 13.2
**Researched:** 2026-09-30

> **Read this first.** There is **no authoritative salary survey for Pakistani accountants.**
> No government dataset, no professional-body survey, no audited industry study was found at
> a usable level of granularity. Everything below is triangulation across weak sources, and
> it is presented that way deliberately. A confident single number here would be fiction.
> See `../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-008.

---

## 1. The one high-confidence anchor: ICAP's mandated stipends

This is the **only Tier-A, legally-binding, currently-dated compensation data** found for any
Pakistani accounting credential. Everything else should be calibrated against it.

**ICAP minimum stipend rates, effective 1 July 2026** (PKR/month):

| Trainee stage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| On commencement of training (CAF passed, 3.5-yr route) | **27,500** |
| On commencement (other quals, incl. 14-yr associate degree, 4-yr route) | **24,200** |
| On commencement (SDAI/RDAI graduates) | **34,100** |
| On passing four CFAP/MSA papers or more | **38,500** |
| **Qualified**, more than six months of training remaining | **55,000** |
| **Qualified**, six months or less remaining | **93,500** |

Source: ICAP, Stipend Rates — https://icap.org.pk/students/training/stipend-rates/ (Tier A,
accessed 2026-09-30); increase confirmed by ProPakistani, 1 Jul 2026 (Tier C).

### Why this anchor matters more than any aggregator figure

`93,500/month` is what a **fully-qualified CA who is still a trainee** must be paid, **as a
regulated floor**, by an authorised training organisation. A real permanent-role salary for a
newly-qualified CA must exceed this or nobody would leave training. So:

> **Newly-qualified CA (ICAP) in Pakistan: > PKR 93,500/month, with high confidence.**

That is a genuinely defensible statement. Almost nothing else in this document is.

## 4. Why the brief's CMA figures must be rejected

The brief states: entry PKR 2,686,111/yr (≈224K/month); mid PKR 3,781,672/yr (≈315K/month);
senior PKR 4,774,005/yr (≈398K/month), with "+57% over 5 years".

**Five independent reasons to reject:**

1. **Precision is fake.** "PKR 2,686,111" implies rupee-level accuracy on a national average.
   No survey produces that. This precision pattern is the signature of a **cost-of-living
   salary model** (ERI/SalaryExpert-type), which converts US benchmark salaries by
   purchasing-power factors. It is a *model output*, not an observation.
2. **It exceeds ICAP's regulated CA floor by 2.4×** at entry. CA is universally the
   highest-paid entry credential in Pakistan; CMA entry cannot exceed it.
3. **The brief contradicts itself** — Area 5.1 says CMA starting salary is 80K–120K/month.
4. **No Tier-C Pakistani source comes close.** The highest commercial estimate found is
   120,000–200,000; most say 60,000–90,000.
5. **The "+57% over 5 years" growth rate is *below* Pakistani inflation** for much of
   2022–2024. A figure presented as career progress that is actually a real-terms pay cut
   suggests the underlying model is not tracking Pakistani conditions at all.

**Decision:** rejected and excluded from all Learms-facing outputs. Recorded in
`../CONTRADICTIONS.md` C-005 and `../DECISIONS-7.md` D-004.

## 5. The claim that salaries are "stagnant"

The brief asserts salaries are "same as 7-8 years ago". **Directionally supportable, but the
right frame is real terms, not nominal.**

What can be shown from Tier-A data: ICAP raised trainee stipends ~10% in July 2026
(25,000 → 27,500 at commencement). ICMAP raised fees ~5% and ICAP ~10% over the same period.

The honest statement is: **nominal entry salaries have risen; whether they have risen faster
than Pakistani CPI over 2018–2026 could not be established from sources retrieved in this
session.** Given the 2022–2023 inflation episode, real-terms erosion is highly likely, but
**Learms should not assert it without the CPI series.** Recorded as a gap.

## 6. How Learms should present salary information

1. **Never show a single average.** Show a range with an explicit confidence label.
2. **Lead with the ICAP stipend table** for CA — it is regulated, dated, and verifiable, and
   it is the only number on any Pakistani accounting site that is actually guaranteed.
3. **Show the source and its date** next to every figure. A 2024 figure in 2026 Pakistan is
   materially stale.
4. **Separate part-qualified / affiliate / qualified.** Most confusion in this space comes
   from comparing an ACCA affiliate to a qualified CA.
5. **Separate ICMAP CMA from IMA USA CMA.** They are routinely conflated and the markets
   differ.
6. **Do not use Glassdoor, SalaryExpert, ERI or Payscale for Pakistan.** Documented failures
   above.
7. **State the sample size** when known. "n = 22 self-reported" is important context.
