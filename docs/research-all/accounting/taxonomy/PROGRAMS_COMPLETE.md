## 0. The naming collisions that break search and content systems

Before the list: **three name collisions** cause real damage in any accounting content
product, and Learms must handle them explicitly.

### Collision 1 — "CMA" means three different credentials

| Body | Country | Designation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICMAP / ICMA International** | Pakistan | **Chartered** Management Accountant (CMA) | Historically "Cost and Management Accountant". 18 courses + 6 PTM. |
| **IMA** | USA | **Certified** Management Accountant (CMA) | 2 parts. Globally portable. |
| **ICMAI** | India | Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) | Foundation / Intermediate / Final. |
| **ICMAB** | Bangladesh | Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) | Knowledge / Operational / Intermediate / Advanced levels. |

A Pakistani student searching "CMA syllabus" or "CMA salary" will get IMA-USA and ICMAI-India
results far more often than ICMAP results, because those bodies have larger content
ecosystems. **Most "CMA Pakistan" salary content found in this research is silently
contaminated with IMA-USA data.** See `../cma-deep/CMA_PAKISTAN_VS_USA.md`.

### Collision 2 — "ICMAP" also denotes an Indonesian university programme

A peer-reviewed paper titled *"ICMAP Students' Problems and Solutions in Learning English"*
(IntechOpen / ResearchGate, 2024) is about the **International Class of Management and
Accounting Program**, an Indonesian undergraduate programme — **not** the Institute of Cost
and Management Accountants of Pakistan. Any automated literature ingestion keyed on "ICMAP"
will pull this in and mis-attribute its findings to Pakistani cost accountants.

Sources: https://www.intechopen.com/online-first/83654 ;
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383766626_ICMAP_Students'_Problems_and_Solutions_in_Learning_English

### Collision 3 — ICMAP's own naming is unstable

"ICMAP" (statutory), "ICMA International" (current branding), "Cost and Management
Accountant" (historic designation), "Chartered Management Accountant" (Study Scheme-2025
designation) — plus **CIMA UK** also awards "Chartered Management Accountant". Index all
forms or students will not find their own qualification.

## 1. Qualifications available to / relevant in Pakistan

### 1.1 Pakistani statutory bodies

| # | Qualification | Body | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CMA / AFA** — Chartered Management Accountant, Affiliate Financial Accountant | **ICMAP / ICMA International** | https://www.icmap.com.pk/ |
| 2 | **CA** — Chartered Accountant | **ICAP** | https://icap.org.pk/ |
| 3 | **APFA / AFA** — Public Finance Accountant | **PIPFA** — Pakistan Institute of Public Finance Accountants | https://www.pipfa.org.pk/ |

**PIPFA note:** PIPFA is jointly sponsored by ICAP and ICMAP and functions as the
technician-tier feeder. Its strategic importance rose sharply in 2026: **ICAP cut its
exemption fee for PIPFA-qualified candidates from PKR 13,500 to PKR 7,400 (a 45% cut)** while
raising the foreign-body exemption fee from 154,000 to 200,500. That is a deliberate policy
signal favouring the domestic PIPFA → CA pipeline.
Source: ICAP Fee page, w.e.f. 1 July 2026 — https://icap.org.pk/students/fees-and-forms/fees/

### 1.2 Foreign professional bodies with a large Pakistani presence

| # | Qualification | Body | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **ACCA** — Chartered Certified Accountant | ACCA (UK) | https://www.accaglobal.com/pk/en.html |
| 5 | **CIMA** / **CGMA** — Chartered Global Management Accountant | CIMA (UK), with AICPA | https://www.aicpa-cima.com/ |
| 6 | **CMA (USA)** — Certified Management Accountant | IMA (USA) | https://www.imanet.org/ |
| 7 | **CPA (USA)** — Certified Public Accountant | AICPA / NASBA / state boards | https://www.aicpa-cima.com/ |
| 8 | **CFA** — Chartered Financial Analyst | CFA Institute | https://www.cfainstitute.org/ |

**ICMAP ↔ ACCA/CIMA/CPA Canada pathways exist and are priced** (Tier A, ICMAP fee page):
- **Membership Pathway Program (MPP) for ACCA-UK Fellow members**: registration 13,200 +
  Membership Pathway Examination 73,700 + Legal Module Examination 20,400.
- **Local Laws Gateway (LLG) for CIMA-UK members**: ACMA examination 63,500; FCMA 53,200.
- **Membership Pathway for CPA Canada members**: PCTM-4 & 5 admission 32,600, exam 11,800,
  waiver fee 120,200.

**ICAP exemption fees by prior body** (Tier A, 2026-27): PIPFA 7,400 · CIMA 37,000 ·
**ICMAP 59,200** · ICAEW/ICAI/ICAS/CAANZ/CPA Canada 200,500.

**Finding:** the ICMAP → ICAP exemption at PKR 59,200 is real and priced, meaning **ICMAP
qualification has a defined, monetised conversion value into CA.** This "CMA then CA" ladder
is rarely presented as a strategy and is a legitimate Learms content angle.

| # | Qualification | Body | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **CIA** — Certified Internal Auditor | The Institute of Internal Auditors | https://www.theiia.org/ |
| 10 | **CISA** — Certified Information Systems Auditor | ISACA | https://www.isaca.org/ |
| 11 | **CFE** — Certified Fraud Examiner | Association of Certified Fraud Examiners | https://www.acfe.com/ |
| 12 | **FRM** — Financial Risk Manager | Global Association of Risk Professionals | https://www.garp.org/ |
| 13 | **CAIA** — Chartered Alternative Investment Analyst | CAIA Association | https://caia.org/ |
| 14 | **CFP** — Certified Financial Planner | CFP Board (US) / FPSB internationally | https://www.cfp.net/ |
| 15 | **EA** — Enrolled Agent | US Internal Revenue Service | https://www.irs.gov/ |
| 16 | **CertIFR / DipIFR** | ACCA | https://www.accaglobal.com/ |

**Relevance filter for Pakistan.** Not all of these are equally sensible for a Pakistani
student, and Learms should say so rather than listing them neutrally:

| Tier | Qualifications | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Core domestic** | CA (ICAP), CMA (ICMAP), PIPFA | Statutory recognition, local practice rights, domestic employer literacy |
| **Core international-in-Pakistan** | ACCA | Large Pakistani presence, Big-4 literate, portable |
| **Genuinely useful add-ons** | CIA, CISA, CFE, DipIFR | Map to real Pakistani job functions (internal audit, IS audit, forensic, IFRS reporting) |
| **High-value but narrow** | CFA, FRM | Concentrated in asset management, banking treasury, risk — a small Pakistani market |
| **Low relevance for most** | **EA**, CFP (US), CAIA | **EA is US federal tax representation** — essentially no Pakistani domestic application except for outsourcing/BPO serving US clients. CFP is a US/other-jurisdiction personal-financial-planning credential with limited Pakistani regulatory meaning. |

⚠️ The brief listed EA, CFP and CAIA alongside CA and ACCA without distinction. **Presenting
EA as a peer option to CA for a Pakistani school-leaver would be bad advice.** Its only
sensible Pakistani use case is a finance professional working in a US-facing outsourcing
practice.

### 1.5 University degrees

| Degree | Typical duration | Role in the ecosystem |
|---|---|---|
| B.Com | 2 years (14-year) / 4 years (16-year) | **The key ICMAP entry lever** — 14-yr → ML-1, 16-yr → ML-2 |
| BBA / BS Accounting & Finance | 4 years (16-year) | Direct ML-2 entry at ICMAP; ACCA exemptions; ICAP PRC/CAF exemptions for SDAI/RDAI |
| M.Com / MBA Finance | 1.5–2 years | Marginal additional exemption value once 16-year status is reached |
| ADP / Associate Degree | 2 years (14-year) | ICAP: 4-year training route, lowest stipend band (PKR 24,200) |

**The degree decision is economically decisive and is usually framed wrongly.** A student
choosing between "start ICMAP now after Intermediate" and "do B.Com first, then ICMAP" faces:

| | Straight from Intermediate | B.Com (16-yr) first |
|---|---|---|
| ICMAP courses | 18 + 6 PTM | 9 + 3 PTM |
| ICMAP registration | 12,500 | 63,000 |
| **ICMAP total (2025 rates)** | **≈727,000** | **≈464,000** |
| Time in ICMAP | 4 years | 2 years |
| Plus | — | 4 years + degree tuition |

The ICMAP-only saving is ≈PKR 263,000 and two years. Whether that beats four years of
university tuition and foregone earnings depends entirely on whether the degree is being
taken anyway. **Learms should compute this, not assert it.** Full working:
`../cma-deep/STUDY_SCHEME_2025_COMPLETE.md` §12.

### 1.6 HEC and degree recognition

Higher Education Commission (HEC) recognition and equivalence determine ICMAP/ICAP entry
routes ("from any HEC recognized university"). ICAP additionally distinguishes **SDAI**
(Selected Degree Awarding Institutes) and **RDAI** (Recognised Degree Awarding Institutes),
which carry **materially higher stipend floors** (PKR 34,100 vs 27,500 at commencement) and
broader PRC/CAF exemptions. HEC: https://www.hec.gov.pk/

**Finding:** the institution a student attends changes their *regulated starting pay* by
~24%. This is a concrete, checkable, high-stakes fact that prospective students are rarely
told before choosing a university.
