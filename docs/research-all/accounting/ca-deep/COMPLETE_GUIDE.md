## 2. Qualification structure — Education and Training Scheme 2025

### 2.1 The full ladder

| Stage | Components |
|---|---|
| **PRC** — Pre-Requisite Competencies | **3 papers** |
| **Hands-on Courses** | **2 courses** (PPE; MS Office for Business) + English Communication Skills |
| **CAF** — Certificate in Accounting and Finance | **8 papers**, split into Group A and Group B |
| **CFAP** — Certified Finance and Accounting Professional | **6 papers** |
| **Integrated Modules** | **2 modules** (AI & Data Analytics; Governance and Ethics) |
| **Strategic Case Study** | **1 paper** |
| **Practical Training** | Mandatory, in an authorised Training Organisation (TO) |

Source: ICAP Education Scheme 2025 FAQs §1.1; Principles and Structure.

### 2.2 CAF papers (8, two groups)

**Group A:** CAF-01 Financial Accounting and Reporting · CAF-02 Taxation Principles and
Compliance · CAF-03 Data, Systems and Risks · (plus one further Group A paper)
**Group B:** the remaining four papers.

⚠️ ICAP's published FAQ names CAF-01 to CAF-03 explicitly in the mapping table; the complete
Group A/Group B split for all eight was **not fully enumerated in the documents retrieved**.
Do not publish a full CAF-01…CAF-08 list from this research — see `../BLOCKERS-7.md` B-010.

Confirmed mappings from Scheme 2021 → Scheme 2025:
| Scheme 2025 | Scheme 2021 |
|---|---|
| CAF-1 Financial Accounting and Reporting | CAF-1 Financial Accounting and Reporting I |
| CAF-2 Taxation Principles and Compliance | CAF-2 Tax Practices |
| CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks | (new composition — maps from a combination of CAF papers) |

### 2.3 CFAP papers (6) — fully confirmed

| Code | Paper | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CFAP-01 | Advanced Corporate Reporting | |
| CFAP-02 | Corporate Laws and Governance | |
| CFAP-03 | **Sustainability Reporting and Assurance** | **NEW in 2025** |
| CFAP-04 | Strategic Business Finance | |
| CFAP-05 | Taxation Practice and Planning | |
| CFAP-06 | Audit, Assurance and Data | |

### 2.4 What was removed

- **MSA-1** and **MSA-2** are replaced by a single **Strategic Case Study** paper, "enhancing
  the practical application of technical knowledge and preparing candidates to solve
  real-world business and finance issues".
- **Strategy and Performance Measurement (SPM)** is **removed** entirely, "to avoid
  duplication".

**Strategic Case Study rules:** attempted after passing all CFAP papers, or with only one
paper remaining, subject to other requirements. **There is no limit on the number of attempts
at this paper.**

### 2.5 Legacy-student transition (a hard deadline students must not miss)

Students who still had **MSA-1 or SPM** outstanding get **two additional attempts — Summer
2026 and Winter 2026**. If they have not passed by the end of Winter 2026, they **must instead
pass CFAP-03 Sustainability Reporting and Assurance** to qualify. Students may voluntarily
switch to CFAP-03 any time after the Winter 2025 session.

**This is time-critical as at the research date (September 2026): the Winter 2026 sitting is
the last chance.** A Learms notification/eligibility feature has obvious, immediate value for
this cohort. Flagged to Agent 2 in `../RECOMMENDATIONS.md`.

## 3. Practical training — the decisive structural difference vs CMA and ACCA

Practical training in an authorised **Training Organisation** is mandatory. Existing options
of **3, 3.5 or 4 years** continue unchanged. Scheme 2025 adds two routes:

| Route | Length | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Uninterrupted training | **2.5 years** | Reduced leave. **Candidate may not attempt any examination during this period.** |
| Post-CFAP-entry training | **3 years** | Start after passing ≥4 CFAP papers, or 2 years after passing CAF, whichever comes first. Reduced study leave. |
| Standard | 3 / 3.5 / 4 years | As before |

Indicative stage timings published by ICAP: PRC 2 months · CAF 18 months · CFAP during
training or 12–18 months · total training 30 / 36 / 42 months depending on route.

**This is the single biggest advantage CA holds over CMA in Pakistan**: the qualification has
a paid, structured, audited apprenticeship built into it. ICMAP's equivalent (six Practical
Training Modules) is coursework, not employment — see `../comparison/CMA_ACCA_CA_COMPARISON.md`.

## 4. Stipends — official minimums, and the myth they disprove

ICAP sets **binding minimum stipends** for trainees. Revised rates effective **1 July 2026**:

### CAF-passed or equivalent — 3.5-year training

| Stage | July 2025 | **July 2026** | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| On commencement of training | 25,000 | **27,500** | +2,500 |
| On passing four CFAP/MSA papers or more | 35,000 | **38,500** | +3,500 |
| Qualified, more than six months of training remaining | 50,000 | **55,000** | +5,000 |
| Qualified, six months or less remaining | 85,000 | **93,500** | +8,500 |

### CAF-passed — 2.5-year uninterrupted training

| Stage | July 2025 | **July 2026** |
|---|---|---|
| On commencement | 25,000 | **27,500** |
| After 1 year | 32,000 | **35,200** |
| After 2 years | 40,000 | **44,000** |

### SDAI / RDAI graduates (higher band)

| Stage | July 2025 | **July 2026** |
|---|---|---|
| On commencement | 31,000 | **34,100** |
| After 1 year | 38,000 | **41,800** |
| After 2 years | 45,000 | **49,500** |

### Other qualifications, including 14-year associate degree — 4-year training

| Stage | July 2025 | **July 2026** |
|---|---|---|
| On commencement | 22,000 | **24,200** |
| On passing four CFAP/MSA papers or more | 35,000 | **38,500** |
| Qualified, >6 months remaining | 50,000 | **55,000** |
| Qualified, ≤6 months remaining | 85,000 | **93,500** |

All figures PKR per month. Grandfathering provisions preserve certain pre-July-2025 rates
(Rs 30,700 for three CFAP papers; Rs 42,600 for all CFAP or three CFAP + one MSA;
Rs 84,700 for fully-qualified trainees).

### ⚠️ Finding: the widely-repeated "CA stipend is PKR 10,000–15,000" claim is false

A commercial CA-fees blog states first-year articleship stipend of "PKR 10,000–15,000 per
month" and final period "PKR 25,000–40,000". Against ICAP's own binding minimum schedule, the
**floor** at commencement is **PKR 27,500** and **PKR 93,500** in the final six months
post-qualification. The blog figure is roughly **one third of the legal minimum** and appears
to be several years stale.

**For Learms:** the "low stipend" grievance is real in the sense that PKR 27,500/month is
below a living wage in Karachi or Lahore, and training runs 2.5–4 years. But the numbers in
circulation understate what ICAP actually mandates. Both things need saying. Total stipend
income across a 3.5-year training at minimum rates is roughly **PKR 1.3–1.6 million** — which
substantially offsets, and can exceed, the cost of the qualification. **No such offset exists
for ICMAP or ACCA students.** This is the core economic argument for CA and it is usually
made badly.

### 5.1 Effective 1 July 2026 (Autumn 2026) — current

| Item | PKR |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Assessment Test (QAT) fee | 6,400 |
| Annual subscription | 6,500 |
| Registration as Full Time Student | 22,000 |
| Registration as Training on Probation | 26,700 |
| Transfer fee for trainee student | 26,400 |
| PRC — per paper | 6,100 |
| CAF — per paper | 7,400 |
| CFAP — per paper | 11,300 |
| Hands-on Courses — per course | 7,500 |
| Integrated Module — per module | 7,500 |
| Strategic Case Study | 31,900 |

**Examination fees (separate):**

| Item | PKR |
|---|---|
| PRC — 1 paper | 7,500 (each additional paper 7,500) |
| CAF/GCC — 1 paper | 7,000 (each additional paper 4,600) |
| CFAP — 1 paper | 14,400 (each additional paper 5,700) |
| Multi Subject Assessment (MSA-1, legacy) | 20,500 |
| Strategic Case Study | 31,900 |

**Note the steep marginal discount:** a CAF candidate sitting one paper pays 7,000; sitting
four pays 7,000 + 3 × 4,600 = **20,800**, i.e. 5,200/paper. ICAP's fee structure actively
rewards sitting multiple papers per attempt. ICMAP's does not (flat per-course fee).
This is a concrete, quantified strategy difference Learms can surface in a planner.

### 5.2 Exemption fees (effective 1 July 2026)

| Prior qualification | PKR |
|---|---|
| PIPFA qualified | 7,400 |
| **ICMAP qualified** | **59,200** |
| CIMA qualified | 37,000 |
| ICAEW / ICAI / ICAS / CAANZ / CPA Canada qualified | 200,500 |
| Exemption from three papers | 22,200 |
| Exemption from four papers | 29,600 |

Year-on-year comparison (2025-26 → 2026-27): PIPFA **13,500 → 7,400** (a 45% *cut*);
ICMAP 54,000 → 59,200; CIMA 40,500 → 37,000; foreign CA bodies 154,000 → 200,500 (+30%).

**Finding:** ICAP sharply reduced the cost of the PIPFA route while raising the cost of the
foreign-body route. That is a visible policy signal favouring the domestic technician-level
pipeline. Relevant to any Learms content that advises on qualification sequencing.

## 6. Financial support — ICAP is materially better resourced than ICMAP here

### CAT–SFS (Students Financial Support)

Eligibility: attending tuition at a Registered Accounting Education Tutor (RAET); **≥80% in
HSSC or equivalent**; **total family income < PKR 100,000/month**; approved by RAET for ≥75%
tuition discount; not receiving another ICAP scholarship.

Benefits: **minimum 75% tuition-fee discount** through the RAET up to CAF level including
hands-on courses (once per subject); **registration fee reduced to Rs 2,000** with the
remainder deferred to the CFAP stage in three instalments; **PRC and CAF examination/exemption
fee reduced to Rs 1,000** once per subject.

### Students Endowment Fund

Merit gates: at CAF level, after the first attempt, all papers passed at first attempt or a
maximum of one failure. At CFAP level, a maximum of two failures at CAF level for the first
attempt; thereafter all papers at first attempt or at most one failure. Applicant must be
financially deserving (assessed by **Ihsan Trust**) and not receiving another ICAP scholarship.

**Finding for Learms:** ICAP publishes named, means-tested, income-thresholded financial
support with specific numbers. **No comparable published scheme was found for ICMAP students**
during this research (see `../solutions/FINANCIAL_SOLUTIONS.md` and `../BLOCKERS-7.md`). For a
student choosing between CA and CMA on affordability grounds, this asymmetry is decisive and
almost never mentioned in comparison content.

## 7. Attempt limits and time limits

- **CFAP time limit:** students must pass at least four CFAP papers within **ten years** from
  the date of commencement of training. (Members' Handbook Part IV, Directive 1.01.)
- **Minimum papers per sitting at CFAP:** unless fewer remain, a student must attempt a
  **minimum of two papers** at CFAP level.
- **Maximum papers per sitting:** CAF is capped at **4 papers**; PRC, GCC and CFAP have no
  stated maximum; there is **no minimum** number of papers requirement at any stage under
  Scheme 2025.
- **Strategic Case Study:** unlimited attempts.
- CFAP eligibility requires **12 months of training completed** on or before the first day of
  the examination month, and all CAF papers passed or exempted.

## 8. CA student problems — evidence and honest gaps

### 8.1 What is documented

| Problem | Evidence | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Training-and-study collision | ICAP itself acknowledges it: the 2.5-year uninterrupted route **forbids sitting any exam** during training, and the 3-year route comes with "reduced study leaves". A scheme that must explicitly trade exam access against training time is describing the conflict. | A (inferred from scheme design) |
| Stipend below living cost | PKR 27,500/month at commencement (July 2026) against Pakistani urban living costs. | A for the figure; the adequacy judgement is mine |
| Long duration | 30–42 months of training plus exams; PRC 2 months + CAF 18 months before training even starts on some routes. | A |
| Ten-year CFAP cliff | Directive 1.01: four CFAP papers within ten years of training commencement or debarment applies. | A |
| Winter 2026 legacy deadline | MSA-1/SPM holders have two sittings left, then forced onto CFAP-03. | A |

### 8.2 What could NOT be established

- **Dropout rates.** ICAP does not publish cohort attrition. The brief's "high dropout rates"
  is plausible and universally asserted but **not sourced**. Recorded in `../BLOCKERS-7.md`.
- **"Shortage of 40,000–50,000 chartered accountants."** Widely repeated in Pakistani media
  and LinkedIn commentary. **No primary source located** — no ICAP publication, no government
  workforce study, no named methodology. Treated as an unsourced claim, not a fact. See
  `../CONTRADICTIONS.md` C-012 and `../pakistan/JOB_MARKET_ANALYSIS.md`.
- **Pass rates by paper.** ICAP publishes results but a clean, current per-paper pass-rate
  series was not retrieved in this session. ACCA publishes this openly; ICAP and ICMAP do not
  make it equally easy. Noted as a gap.

## 9. Salary expectations after qualification

See `../career/SALARY_DATA_2025_2026.md` for the full triangulation and caveats. Short version:
the highest-confidence anchor for *any* Pakistani accounting credential is ICAP's own
**stipend floor of PKR 93,500/month for a qualified trainee in their last six months** — this
is a **regulated minimum for someone not yet in a permanent role**, so genuine post-
qualification market salaries start above it. That single Tier-A datapoint is more useful than
any aggregator estimate, and it is the anchor Learms should use.
