# CPSP FCPS Examination and Training Pathway

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Scope:** General pathway evidence; specialty prospectus and current exam notification remain controlling for an individual candidate

## Topic

FCPS-I entry examination.

### Source

CPSP FCPS-I examination guidelines, National Residency Program booklet, and current FAQ. [MED-033, MED-079, MED-080]

### Key Finding

CPSP's reviewed pathway requires an MBBS degree, or BDS for dental FCPS, completion of one year of recognized house job, valid registration, and FCPS-I passage or an officially granted exemption before normal residency induction. [MED-033]

The current FCPS-I guidelines describe two papers, each with 100 MCQs. Each online paper in Pakistan is two hours; conventional examinations abroad are listed as two and a half hours per paper. [MED-079]

FCPS-I passage is an entry requirement, not a residency appointment. A candidate still needs selection into a training slot and timely RTMC registration.

### Relevance to Learms

Separate `FCPS-I passed` from `inducted`, `RTMC registered`, `accredited site`, and `approved supervisor`. Do not claim that an exam pass guarantees a paid residency.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *FCPS-I Examination Guidelines*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-079.
College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *National Residency Program Booklet*. 2022. MED-033.

## Topic

Residency and Intermediate Module.

### Source

CPSP National Residency Program booklet, current FCPS program page, and FAQ. [MED-031, MED-033, MED-080]

### Key Finding

CPSP describes first-fellowship training as four to five years depending on discipline. The National Residency Program describes induction twice yearly, in January and July. For programs with an Intermediate Module (IMM), the broad sequence is two years of core training, IMM theory/TOACS, then two or three years of advanced training. [MED-031, MED-033]

Current FAQ wording says a candidate normally begins RTMC-registered FCPS-II training within three years of FCPS-I, and that programs with IMM require passing both theory and TOACS within six consecutive attempts—availed or unavailed—starting from completion of two years' training. Specialty rules and formally granted permissions may alter a candidate's case. [MED-080]

### Relevance to Learms

Provide deadline alerts from verified enrollment and training dates, not from self-reported memory alone. “Six consecutive attempts” includes unavailed attempts under the cited FAQ wording and therefore cannot be modeled as six exam sittings.

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *National Residency Program Booklet*. 2022. MED-033.
College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Exam FAQ*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-080.

## Topic

FCPS-II eligibility and assessment.

### Source

CPSP National Residency Program booklet and current FAQ. [MED-033, MED-080]

### Key Finding

The broad FCPS-II eligibility model includes:

- completion of the required four- or five-year RTMC-registered training, including rotations;
- IMM pass where prescribed;
- approved research output under the applicable route;
- required workshop certificates;
- completed supervisor-approved e-logbook;
- specialty-specific requirements.

The current FAQ says a dissertation acceptance letter is mandatory to appear in FCPS-II and gives a general minimum of 200 approved e-log entries: 150 core and 50 rotational. The NRP booklet describes written papers using MCQs or SAQs depending on specialty and a clinical examination including TOACS, with some disciplines also using long cases. [MED-033, MED-080]

These general statements do not replace the candidate's specialty prospectus or current sitting notification.

### Relevance to Learms

A checklist must be specialty-versioned. It should link evidence for each requirement and avoid telling a candidate they are eligible based only on elapsed years.

## Topic

2026 CPSP theory calendar.

### Source

CPSP official examinations schedule. [MED-078]

### Key Finding

The current schedule page lists:

| Examination | 2026 theory dates |
|---|---|
| FCPS-I | 5 Jan; 7 Apr; 7 Jul; 6 Oct |
| IMM | 16 Feb; 4 Aug |
| FCPS-II major subjects | 16 Feb; 29 Sep |
| FCPS-II other subjects | 10 Feb; 4 Aug |

The page describes theory schedules. Clinical/TOACS dates may be communicated separately and must not be inferred from the theory date.

### Relevance to Learms

Calendar entries need component (`theory`, `TOACS`, `clinical`) and status. A single “FCPS exam date” field is unsafe.

## Topic

Attempt validity and clinical attempts.

### Source

CPSP current FAQ. [MED-080]

### Key Finding

For FCPS-II clinical examination after theory passage, CPSP's FAQ says the candidate has three attempts across four consecutive examinations, must appear in the first clinical examination, and may skip one of the remaining three. Absence from the first clinical counts as an attempt. The FAQ also says grace marks are not permitted and that paid “rechecking” is recounting only, not re-evaluation of the paper. [MED-080]

### Relevance to Learms

Attempt tracking is high stakes. Require official result and schedule evidence, show skipped windows, and never infer remaining attempts from a candidate's number of physical attendances alone.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Exam FAQ*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-080.

## Evidence-safe preparation model

1. Pin discipline and current prospectus.
2. Pin candidate stage: FCPS-I, induction, IMM, FCPS-II theory, TOACS/clinical.
3. Map learning objectives and assessment formats from official documents.
4. Track workshops, rotations, research, and logbook completion as verified requirements—not study content.
5. Never use copied recalled exam questions or unauthorized material; CPSP rules prohibit unfair means.
6. Do not publish pass-rate or competitiveness claims: no standardized current specialty-level official dataset was found.
