# National Registration Examination (NRE) — Medical and Dental

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Controlling syllabus version reviewed:** PM&DC 2024 standards, structure, and syllabus

## Topic

Purpose, population, and legal role.

### Source

PM&DC Act 2022 section 18 and PM&DC 2024 NRE standards. [MED-064, MED-073, MED-074]

### Key Finding

NRE is the Pakistan licensing examination route addressed to Pakistani graduates of foreign medical or dental programs. Passing NRE is mandatory before full PM&DC registration unless PM&DC formally applies a lawful exemption. The Act requires PM&DC to conduct NRE at least twice yearly and places no limit on complete-NRE attempts. [MED-064]

The 2024 medical standard describes eligibility as an MBBS/MD or approved equivalent from a PM&DC-approved foreign medical college plus PM&DC provisional license. The dental standard analogously identifies a BDS or approved equivalent from a PM&DC-approved foreign dental college plus provisional license. [MED-073, MED-074]

NRE is not MDCAT, NEB, a house-job assessment, or a postgraduate entrance test.

### Relevance to Learms

Route by learner state: applicant, foreign student, foreign graduate, provisionally registered graduate, NRE theory pass, NRE clinical pass, and full registration are separate nodes.

### Citation

Government of Pakistan. *Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act, 2022*, section 18. MED-064.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *NRE Standard, Structure & Syllabus for Medical Graduates 2024* and corresponding Dental standard. 30 April 2024. MED-073 and MED-074.

## Topic

Medical NRE structure and pass rules.

### Source

PM&DC NRE Medical Standard, Structure & Syllabus 2024. [MED-073]

### Key Finding

| Component | 2024 medical standard |
|---|---|
| Theory | 200 higher-cognition MCQs; substantially computer-based |
| Theory mix | 70% clinical sciences (140); 30% applied basic sciences (60) |
| Theory pass | 60%, with Angoff standardization also stated |
| Clinical | CSE similar to an OSCE |
| CSE pass | 60% and pass all three mandatory stations |
| Negative marking | None |
| Sequence | Theory pass required before CSE |
| Theory validity | 5 years |
| CSE failure rule | After three consecutive CSE failures, retake theory and CSE on the fourth attempt |

The clinical allocation in the source includes medicine/allied 52 MCQs, surgery/allied 48, obstetrics and gynaecology 14, paediatrics 14, ophthalmology 6, and ENT 6. The source contains a likely table transcription inconsistency in the basic-science line items: displayed rows sum to 56 although the heading and total state 60. Learms must retain the official total and flag the row-level mismatch rather than manufacture four questions.

### Relevance to Learms

Use the official syllabus objectives and declared domain totals, with a visible source-conflict marker on the basic-science row sum. Do not create a false precise study weighting from inconsistent rows.

## Topic

Dental NRE structure and pass rules.

### Source

PM&DC NRE Dental Standard, Structure & Syllabus 2024. [MED-074]

### Key Finding

Dental NRE has a 200-MCQ theory component followed by a two-session CSE: session 1 has 15 stations; session 2 has 3 stations of 50 minutes each. The 2024 standard sets 60% for theory and 60% combined CSE, while requiring all three mandatory stations. There is no negative marking. Theory remains valid for five years, and after three consecutive CSE failures the candidate must retake the complete NRE. [MED-074]

The theory table allocates all 200 questions across dental materials, pharmacology/therapeutics, community/preventive dentistry, general medicine/surgery, periodontology, oral medicine/diagnosis/radiology, operative dentistry including paediatric dentistry and endodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, and orthodontics.

### Relevance to Learms

Medical and dental NRE cannot share one exam blueprint. Keep profession-specific theory and clinical nodes.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *National Registration Examination Standard, Structure & Syllabus for Dental Graduates 2024*. Notification dated 30 April 2024. MED-074.

## Topic

Why old NRE preparation claims can be wrong.

### Source

PM&DC 2023 and 2024 NRE standards. [MED-073, MED-074, MED-085, MED-086]

### Key Finding

PM&DC's 2023 standards used a 70% theory and CSE threshold. The later 2024 medical and dental standards use 60% and state that Angoff's method will also be adopted. Therefore, unsourced notes that say “NRE pass mark is 70%” are stale for the reviewed 2024 standard. This research does not infer whether a later exam-specific notice adjusts the standard; candidates must check the notification for their sitting.

### Relevance to Learms

Exam facts require `standard_version` and `sitting_notice`. The product should expire old flashcards or guidance when a regulator changes pass rules.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. NRE standards for medical and dental graduates, 2023 and 2024. MED-073, MED-074, MED-085, and MED-086.

## Topic

Current 2026 sitting evidence.

### Source

PM&DC notice dated 21 August 2026. [MED-075]

### Key Finding

PM&DC scheduled NRE Step-I for **11 October 2026** through National University of Medical Sciences, Rawalpindi, with the registration portal stated to run from 27 August through 10 September 2026. [MED-075]

At the evidence cutoff, a matching current Step-II date and a full sitting-specific candidate notification were **not found** in the reviewed sources. The 2024 syllabus describes the standard, while the 2026 notice establishes the dated sitting; neither should substitute for the other.

### Relevance to Learms

## Safe study architecture

1. Verify personal eligibility and provisional-license status directly with PM&DC.
2. Pin the current standard and sitting notice.
3. Map objectives to applied basic/clinical or dental domains.
4. Practice higher-cognition MCQs without treating recall volume as competence.
5. Practice CSE/OSCE communication, examination, interpretation, management, procedures, safety, and mandatory-station tasks.
6. Never use real patient-identifiable material in practice logs.
