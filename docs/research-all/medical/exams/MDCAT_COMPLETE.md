# MDCAT — Current Entry-Test Evidence

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Current-cycle caution:** PM&DC moved MDCAT 2026 from 16 August to 20 September 2026. The scheduled date has elapsed, but completion, results, and any later directions were not verified in this checkpoint.

## Topic

Legal role of MDCAT.

### Source

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act 2022, section 17, and PM&DC 2025 admissions regulations. [MED-002, MED-064]

### Key Finding

The Act requires each province, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Islamabad Capital Territory to conduct a single admission test on dates approved by PM&DC for applicants to public and private undergraduate medical and dental programs. The Act says MDCAT contributes at least 50% of admission weight and that a provincial MDCAT result is valid nationally for three years, while allowing domicile preference. The 2025 admissions regulations use 50% MDCAT, 40% HSSC/equivalent, and 10% SSC/equivalent for the reviewed public and private merit calculation. [MED-002, MED-064]

Passing MDCAT establishes only one eligibility component. It does not create a right to admission and does not replace HSSC/equivalence, subject, domicile, category, merit, preference, or seat requirements.

### Relevance to Learms

Model `exam_pass`, `exam_score`, `result_validity`, `admission_eligibility`, and `selection` separately. A “passed” badge must not become “likely admitted.”

### Citation

Government of Pakistan. *Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act, 2022*, Act IV of 2023, section 17. Gazette dated 16 January 2023. MED-064.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Medical and Dental Undergraduate Education (Admissions, Curriculum and Conduct) Policy and Regulations 2025*. MED-002.

## Topic

MDCAT 2026 format and conducting authorities.

### Source

PM&DC MDCAT-2026 registration notice. [MED-070]

### Key Finding

The 2026 notice specified a paper-based, English-language test with 180 MCQs, no negative marking, and this distribution:

| Domain | Questions | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Biology | 81 | 45% |
| Chemistry | 45 | 25% |
| Physics | 36 | 20% |
| English | 9 | 5% |
| Logical reasoning | 9 | 5% |
| **Total** | **180** | **100%** |

It described difficulty as 15% easy, 70% moderate, and 15% difficult. The named regional test/conducting universities were UHS for Punjab, Sukkur IBA for Sindh, KMU for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, BUMHS for Balochistan, and SZABMU for ICT, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Riyadh.

The notice initially set 16 August 2026; PM&DC later moved the examination to 20 September 2026 while stating that other terms and arrangements remained unchanged unless further notified. [MED-070, MED-071]

### Relevance to Learms

Every study plan and countdown must carry a cycle identifier and source date. Calendar changes must append a superseding event rather than overwrite the original notice without history.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Public Notice: MDCAT-2026 for Admissions in MBBS/BDS Programs*. 2026. MED-070.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *MDCAT Previously Scheduled for 16th August 2026 Rescheduled to 20th September 2026*. 6 August 2026. MED-071.

## Topic

Syllabus and passing marks.

### Source

PM&DC's final uniform MDCAT curriculum published for 2025 and PM&DC's 2026 notice. [MED-070, MED-072]

### Key Finding

The latest final curriculum located in PM&DC's syllabus index was titled **MDCAT 2025**. It matches the 2026 notice's 180-question subject distribution and states a three-hour duration, no negative marking, 55% minimum pass marks for medical-college admission, and 50% for dental-college admission. [MED-072]

The 2026 notice points users to the PM&DC curriculum/syllabus but does not embed a version number in the fetched text. Therefore, the 2025 curriculum is the latest final syllabus located and is strongly consistent with the 2026 pattern, but the repository does not relabel the PDF itself as “MDCAT 2026.”

### Relevance to Learms

The content graph may use the 2025 learning outcomes for a 2026-aligned study view only with this provenance note. If PM&DC publishes a newly versioned final syllabus, the mapping must be refreshed.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Medical and Dental Colleges Admission Test Curriculum 2025*. Final 26 May 2025. <https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Syllabus/Uniform%20Curriculum%20MDCAT-2025%20%20Final%20(26-05-2025).pdf>. MED-072.

## Topic

Registration, centre, and date mutability.

### Source

PM&DC 2026 opening, extension, centre, and rescheduling notices. [MED-070, MED-071]

### Key Finding

The original 2026 notice gave registration dates and fees, but PM&DC later extended registration deadlines and added/clarified centre arrangements before rescheduling the examination. This sequence demonstrates that registration deadline, fee, centre, and test date are event records, not evergreen exam properties.

As of the evidence cutoff, an official completion/result notice for the scheduled 20 September test was **not found in this checkpoint**. Learms should not infer that a scheduled examination occurred exactly as planned merely because the date passed.

### Relevance to Learms

Use status values such as `announced`, `rescheduled`, `registration_extended`, `completed_verified`, and `result_published`; never derive completion from system time alone.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council, MDCAT-2026 notices, MED-070 and MED-071, accessed 2026-09-30.

## Learms prerequisite map

`HSSC subjects/equivalence -> MDCAT learning outcomes -> MDCAT result -> admission eligibility -> session/category merit -> selection`

These are ordered process stages, not evidence that every syllabus topic is a cognitive prerequisite for every later medical concept.
