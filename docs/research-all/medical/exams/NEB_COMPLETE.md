# National Equivalence Board Examination (NEB) — Transfer from Foreign Study

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Controlling standards reviewed:** PM&DC 2024-and-onwards medical and dental NEB syllabi

## Topic

Purpose and boundary between NEB and NRE.

### Source

PM&DC Act 2022 section 19 and PM&DC NEB standards. [MED-064, MED-076, MED-077]

### Key Finding

NEB assesses Pakistani students who partially completed a medical or dental program abroad and seek transfer to a Pakistani college. NRE addresses foreign graduates seeking full practitioner registration. The Act requires more than two years of foreign study and says the student must take NEB within 12 months of leaving the foreign program. [MED-064]

Passing NEB does not guarantee transfer. A PM&DC-recognized Pakistani college must have an available seat, and the student remains subject to its admission/migration policy and approved seat allocation. [MED-076, MED-077]

### Relevance to Learms

Route users by `partially_completed_foreign_program` versus `foreign_graduate`; presenting NRE preparation to an NEB candidate is a material pathway error.

### Citation

Government of Pakistan. *Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act, 2022*, section 19. MED-064.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *NEB Examinations 2024 (Standards, Structure & Syllabus)* for medical and dental students. MED-076 and MED-077.

## Topic

Shared NEB rules in the 2024 standards.

### Source

PM&DC medical and dental NEB standards, notified 23 January 2024. [MED-076, MED-077]

### Key Finding

Both standards state:

- more than two years of the relevant foreign program must have been completed;
- the first attempt must occur within 12 months of leaving the foreign program;
- NEB is held at least twice yearly;
- no attempt limit applies after satisfying the first-attempt timing condition;
- the result is valid for two years, and the highest result during that period may be used;
- theory has 200 application-focused, substantially computer-based MCQs over three hours;
- there is no negative marking;
- transfer depends on a vacant approved seat and admission policy.

The 2024 standards say their communicated results are final and not available for rechecking or objection. A learner-facing explanation should quote this carefully rather than promise an appeal route.

### Relevance to Learms

NEB records require `target_year`, because blueprint and clinical-component requirements differ by transfer year.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. NEB Medical and Dental Standards, Structure & Syllabus 2024 and onwards. MED-076 and MED-077.

## Topic

Medical NEB standards by target year.

### Source

PM&DC NEB Medical Standard 2024 and onwards. [MED-076]

### Key Finding

| Target | Prior foreign year required | Exam components | Pass rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBBS Year 3 | Passed Year 2 | Theory | 70% theory |
| MBBS Year 4 | Passed Year 3 | Theory | 70% theory |
| MBBS Year 5 | Passed Year 4 | Theory + clinical skills | 70% separately in both |

Standard 3 weights anatomy 45%, physiology 35%, and biochemistry 20%. Standard 4 weights general pathology 25%, general microbiology 25%, and pharmacology 50%. Standard 5 weights special pathology 20%, community medicine 10%, ophthalmology 35%, and ENT 35%.

After three consecutive clinical-skills failures, the source requires the candidate to retake both theory and clinical components on the fourth attempt.

### Relevance to Learms

Do not give one generic “NEB syllabus.” Build target-year plans and require documentary eligibility verification before study scheduling.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *National Equivalence Board Examinations 2024 (Standards, Structure & Syllabus) for Foreign Medical Students*. 23 January 2024. MED-076.

## Topic

Dental NEB standards by target year.

### Source

PM&DC NEB Dental Standard 2024 and onwards. [MED-077]

### Key Finding

| Target | Prior foreign year required | Exam components | Pass rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| BDS Year 3 | Passed Year 2 | Theory | 70% theory |
| BDS Year 4 | Passed Year 3 | Theory + clinical skills | 70% separately in both |

For BDS Year 3, the blueprint covers general pathology, pharmacology including dental therapeutics, dental materials, and oral pathology. For BDS Year 4, it covers oral medicine/diagnosis/radiology, general medicine, general surgery, community/preventive dentistry, and periodontology. [MED-077]

### Relevance to Learms

Keep medical and dental target years as different pathway nodes; do not infer a BDS Year 5 NEB from the medical structure.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *National Equivalence Board Examinations 2024 (Standards, Structure & Syllabus) for Foreign Dental Students*. 23 January 2024. MED-077.

## Topic

Migration-timing conflict.

### Source

PM&DC Registration Regulations 2023 and later NEB 2024 standards. [MED-066, MED-076, MED-077]

### Key Finding

The 2023 Registration Regulations say migration into a particular year is not allowed if more than **three months** have passed since that academic year began; the later 2024 NEB standards say **two months**. The later, exam-specific standards appear more current, but this research does not silently erase the official conflict or offer legal interpretation.

### Relevance to Learms

Show the two-month 2024 rule with a conflict warning and advise direct PM&DC/admitting-college confirmation. Never schedule around the older three-month value without verification.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Registration Regulations 2023*, regulation 24. MED-066.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. NEB 2024 medical and dental standards. MED-076 and MED-077.
