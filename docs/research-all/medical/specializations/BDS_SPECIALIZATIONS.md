# BDS Specializations and Postgraduate Dental Pathways in Pakistan

**Evidence cutoff:** 30 September 2026  
**Scope:** Pakistan; dental postgraduate specialties after BDS  
**Authorities:** PM&DC for professional and postgraduate recognition; CPSP for FCPS/MCPS pathways  
**Status:** Source-grounded pathway map; current specialty-specific prospectuses must control

> A specialty label is not proof of a recognized seat. Verify the exact qualification, training site, awarding body, supervisor, intake, and PM&DC record before enrollment.

## 1. Qualification and specialty are different objects

Dental learners may encounter FCPS, MCPS, MDS, MSc, MPhil, and diploma awards. The award route, specialty, institution, and recognition status must remain separate. “Orthodontics,” for example, is a specialty; FCPS Orthodontics, MCPS Orthodontics, and MDS Orthodontics are distinct qualification-program records. [MED-031; MED-036; MED-039]

## 2. Current CPSP first-fellowship dental inventory

### Source

CPSP current FCPS training-program page. [MED-031]

### Key Finding

Six labels on the current first-fellowship page are explicitly dental:

1. Dentistry (Oral and Maxillo-Facial Surgery)
2. Dentistry (Orthodontics)
3. Dentistry (Periodontology)
4. Dentistry (Prosthodontics)
5. Operative Dentistry and Endodontics
6. Paediatric Dentistry

The page states that first-fellowship duration varies from four to five years by specialty. It does not show a duration beside each dental label.

### Relevance to Learms

Render these as six separately sourced program labels. Do not merge “Operative Dentistry and Endodontics” into either discipline without preserving CPSP's exact combined label.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Fellowship of the College of Physicians and Surgeons—FCPS*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 3. Oral and maxillofacial surgery: documented legacy pathway

### Source

CPSP, *FCPS-II Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery* prospectus (2012). [MED-038]

### Key Finding

The prospectus documents a four-year pathway: two years of RTMC-registered Intermediate Module training and two years of FCPS-II training. It describes rotations that connect dentistry with general surgery, anaesthesia, ENT, neurosurgery, plastic surgery, orthodontics, and prosthodontics. Eligibility for the final examination included FCPS-I Dentistry/exemption, required registered training, IMM, logbook, research, and workshops.

This is a useful curriculum source but is a **2012 document**. The current CPSP program page, registration record, and applicable prospectus must be checked for a new entrant.

### Relevance to Learms

The curriculum supports a competency graph spanning dentoalveolar surgery, trauma, pathology/oncology, reconstruction, anaesthesia, and multidisciplinary rotations. Tag all rules from this prospectus `effective_from = 2012` and `current_status = verify`.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *FCPS-II Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery: Requirements for Training and Examination*. 2012. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/trainee/prospectus/fcps2/FCPS-II%20OMFS.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 4. Other dental fellowships: what is and is not verified

### Source

CPSP current FCPS page, clinical-guideline index, and accredited-institution directory. [MED-031; MED-032; MED-035]

### Key Finding

CPSP's clinical-guideline index provides public links for Operative Dentistry, Oral and Maxillo-Facial Surgery, Orthodontics, and Prosthodontics. The current FCPS page additionally names Periodontology and Paediatric Dentistry. Public clinical-guideline links for those latter two were not visible on the reviewed index. CPSP's institution directory can be searched by specialty, but availability is institution- and specialty-specific.

### Relevance to Learms

Show `official_program_listed`, `public_curriculum_located`, and `accredited_site_located` as different fields. Do not treat a missing public guideline PDF as evidence that a listed program is inactive.

### Citation

CPSP. *FCPS Training Programs*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *Clinical Guidelines*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/clinical-guideline.php (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *FCPS Accredited Institutions*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/accredited-institutes-fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 5. MCPS dental pathways

### Source

CPSP current MCPS training page and MCPS accredited-institution directory. [MED-036; MED-041]

### Key Finding

The current MCPS page states a two-year training duration and visibly lists five dental labels:

- Dentistry (Operative Dentistry)
- Dentistry (Orthodontics)
- Dentistry (Periodontology)
- Dentistry (Prosthodontics)
- Family Dentistry

The separate accredited-institution directory exposes additional labels, including Oral and Maxillo-Facial Surgery and Oral Surgery. The two current CPSP pages therefore do not present an identical taxonomy.

### Relevance to Learms

Use the training-program page as the visible current program inventory but display a source-conflict warning when the accredited directory has additional labels. A directory option must not be promoted to an open/current national training route without confirmation.

### Citation

CPSP. *Membership of the College of Physicians and Surgeons—MCPS*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/mcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *MCPS Accredited Institutions*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/accredited-institutes-mcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 6. University MDS/MSc/MPhil and diploma routes

### Source

PM&DC 2023 postgraduate standards and recognized Pakistani postgraduate qualification register dated 18 August 2026. [MED-042; MED-039]

### Key Finding

The PM&DC register contains institution-specific dental qualifications with labels such as MDS Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, MDS Orthodontics, MDS Operative Dentistry, MDS Oral Pathology, MSc Oral Surgery, MSc Orthodontics, MSc Prosthetics/Prosthodontics, MSc Oral Medicine, MSc Periodontology, MPhil Oral Biology, MPhil Oral Medicine, MPhil Dental Materials, and preventive/community dentistry variants. This is not a statement that every named route is open at every institution or for every cohort.

### Relevance to Learms

Learms should ingest each PM&DC row as a `ProgramRecognition` record. It must not generate a universal list by deduplicating titles and discarding training site or awarding institution.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Postgraduate Medical/Dental Education Standards and Regulations*. 2023. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/law/Approved%20PG%20regulations%202023.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *Recognized Pakistani PGQs 18.08.2026*. 2026. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Others/Recognized%20Pakistani%20PGQs%2018.08.2026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 7. Recognition is cohort- and site-sensitive

### Source

PM&DC postgraduate recognition clarification (27 February 2026). [MED-040]

### Key Finding

PM&DC says entrants after promulgation of the PM&DC Act 2022 must meet recognition requirements for the qualification, awarding institution, and training site. Historic amnesty arrangements are limited and should not be interpreted as current permission to enroll in an unrecognized program.

### Relevance to Learms

An eligibility checker must ask for intended intake date and exact site. A generic university recognition badge is insufficient for a postgraduate dental program.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *PMDC gives relief to MD/MS/PhD degree holders/students*. 27 February 2026. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/press/pg%20press%20release%20md%20ms%20mds%20fcps01032026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 8. Entry and assessment model

### Source

CPSP NRP booklet, training guidelines, and OMFS prospectus. [MED-033; MED-034; MED-038]

### Key Finding

The documented CPSP dental fellowship pathway includes a recognized BDS/equivalent and professional registration, house job or applicable recognized route, FCPS-I Dentistry/exemption, selection into an accredited training post, RTMC registration, supervised training, required rotations, logbook, workshops, research, IMM where prescribed, and final assessment.

### Relevance to Learms

Model at least these states:

`BDS completed → registration/house job verified → FCPS-I Dentistry → institutional selection → RTMC registration → core/IMM → advanced specialty → research/logbook/workshops → FCPS-II`

Not every pathway has identical rotations or assessment. Specialty-specific current documents override this generic sequence.

### Citation

CPSP. *National Residency Program*. 2022. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/dnrp/DNRP_Booklet.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *General Information: Post Graduate Training and Their Requirements*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/general-info.php (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *FCPS-II Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery*. 2012. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/trainee/prospectus/fcps2/FCPS-II%20OMFS.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 9. Domain map for learning, not credential inference

| Dental domain | Typical knowledge/skill nodes | Credential caution |
|---|---|---|
| Oral and maxillofacial surgery | Dentoalveolar surgery, trauma, pathology, reconstruction, perioperative care | Medical rotations do not convert BDS to MBBS |
| Orthodontics | Growth, occlusion, cephalometry, appliances, retention | Keep FCPS/MCPS/MDS routes separate |
| Periodontology | Periodontal diagnosis, non-surgical/surgical therapy, maintenance | Current public FCPS guideline not located |
| Prosthodontics | Fixed/removable prosthodontics, implants, maxillofacial rehabilitation | Award titles vary in PM&DC register |
| Operative dentistry/endodontics | Cariology, restorative treatment, endodontic diagnosis/procedures | Preserve CPSP's combined FCPS label |
| Paediatric dentistry | Child development, prevention, behaviour guidance, paediatric procedures | Current public FCPS guideline not located |
| Oral pathology/medicine/biology | Diagnosis, histopathology, oral-systemic disease, research | Often appears in university routes rather than current CPSP FCPS list |
| Community/preventive dentistry | Population oral health, prevention, epidemiology | Do not merge with Family Dentistry |

This table organizes curricula. It does not claim qualification equivalence or current admission availability.

## 10. Competitiveness, demand, work-life, and earnings

### Source

Reviewed PM&DC and CPSP official sources. [MED-031–MED-041]

### Key Finding

National specialty-by-specialty application ratios, seat cutoffs, pass rates, workforce vacancies, hours, wellbeing indicators, and earnings distributions were **not found** in the reviewed official sources.

### Relevance to Learms

Do not rank dental specialties by “scope,” prestige, difficulty, lifestyle, or income. A later career module should use dated public vacancy/payroll data or transparent survey methods and show province, sector, role, experience, and sample size.

### Citation

CPSP. *FCPS Accredited Institutions*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/accredited-institutes-fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *Recognized Pakistani PGQs 18.08.2026*. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Others/Recognized%20Pakistani%20PGQs%2018.08.2026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 12. Learms recommendations

### For Agent 1: knowledge graph

- Treat `DentalSpecialty`, `Qualification`, `ProgramOffering`, and `RecognitionRecord` as different nodes.
- Preserve CPSP's verbatim labels plus normalized aliases.
- Store `public_curriculum_not_located` separately from `program_status`.
- Attach effective/retrieval dates to every pathway edge.

### For Agent 2: content and UI

- Show “verify current site and cohort” on every postgraduate dental card.
- Provide side-by-side award-route tabs rather than asserting FCPS/MDS equivalence.
- Surface source conflicts where MCPS pages differ.
- Avoid unsourced salary and “best specialty” copy.

### For Agent 3: privacy and safety

- Training logs should contain de-identified competency evidence, not patient case records.
- Oral photographs/radiographs are patient data; do not permit upload into a generic learner portfolio without a lawful clinical workflow, authorization, minimization, retention, and access controls.
