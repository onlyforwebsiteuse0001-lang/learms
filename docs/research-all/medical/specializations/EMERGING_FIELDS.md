# Emerging and Interdisciplinary Health Fields in Pakistan

**Evidence cutoff:** 30 September 2026  
**Scope:** Health informatics, bioinformatics, precision medicine, public-health informatics, education, management, clinical research, AI, and unsupported credential claims  
**Status:** Evidence map; interdisciplinary study does not imply clinical licensure

## 1. “Emerging field” is not a qualification type

An emerging field may be:

- a regulated allied-health discipline;
- a specialization inside a computing or public-health degree;
- a curricular track inside Pharm.D;
- an FCPS/MCPS qualification;
- an academic MS/MPhil/PhD subject;
- a research topic or job function with no nationally standardized credential.

Learms must identify which one applies before showing a pathway.

## 2. Medical Informatics under AHPC

### Source

AHPC current disciplines page. [MED-013]

### Key Finding

AHPC lists **Medical Informatics** as one of its 30 allied-health discipline groups. The reviewed AHPC page does not itself provide a national degree curriculum, specialty levels, or a procedure-level scope for Medical Informatics.

### Relevance to Learms

Create an AHPC discipline node but do not infer that every Health Informatics or computer-science graduate is registered in that discipline. Degree, curriculum, institution, and registration need independent evidence.

### Citation

Allied Health Professionals Council. *Our Disciplines*. https://www.ahpc.org.pk/our-disciplines.html (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 3. Health Informatics as a BS Computer Science specialization

### Source

HEC, *Computer Science Degree-Program Curricula* (2025). [MED-048]

### Key Finding

HEC's current BS Computer Science framework includes Health Informatics among optional specializations. The track's recommended study includes health/public-health informatics, statistics, health data modalities, NLP and machine learning in healthcare, interoperability, privacy/security, governance/ethics, health information systems/EHRs, telehealth, decision support, genomic data, and healthcare project management.

### Relevance to Learms

This route is `BS Computer Science (Health Informatics)`, not MBBS, not a clinical specialty, and not automatically AHPC registration. It is especially relevant to Agent 1's ontology and Agent 3's data-governance design.

### Citation

Higher Education Commission Pakistan. *HEC Curricula for Computer Science Degree Programs*. 2025, Health Informatics specialization. https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2025-2026/COMPUTER-SCIENCE.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 4. Public Health Informatics

### Source

HEC, *Public Health Curriculum* (2025). [MED-017]

### Key Finding

HEC's public-health curriculum includes **Public Health Informatics** as an optional specialization. Recommended courses include interoperability, EHR/public-health links, predictive analytics, machine learning, GIS, privacy/security, mHealth, visualization, big data, cloud computing, and bioinformatics.

### Relevance to Learms

Public Health Informatics and BS Computer Science Health Informatics share concepts but are not the same qualification. The graph can share concept nodes while preserving distinct degree/program paths.

### Citation

Higher Education Commission Pakistan. *Curriculum of Public Health Degree Programs*. 2025, “Specialization 13: Public Health Informatics.” https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2024-2025/Public-Health.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 5. Bioinformatics, genomics, and precision medicine

### Source

HEC, *Bioinformatics Degree-Program Curricula* (2026). [MED-047]

### Key Finding

The 2026 framework covers AD, BS, and MS/MPhil bioinformatics. The BS curriculum includes clinical bioinformatics and permits specialization. One named specialization is **Genomics and Precision Medicine**, with recommended content such as human genome analysis, cancer genomics, pharmacogenomics, population genetics, single-cell sequencing, epigenomics, transcriptomics, variant calling, personalized-medicine bioinformatics, and clinical-trial bioinformatics. Artificial Intelligence is another named specialization.

### Relevance to Learms

These are computational/biological education pathways. They do not authorize diagnosis or treatment. Learms can reuse genomic concepts across medicine, pharmacy, public health, and bioinformatics while enforcing role-specific scope.

### Citation

Higher Education Commission Pakistan. *HEC Curricula for Bioinformatics Degree Programs*. 2026. https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2025-2026/Bioinformatics.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 6. Artificial intelligence in health education

### Source

HEC DPT, Pharm.D, Computer Science, and Bioinformatics curricula. [MED-005; MED-006; MED-047; MED-048]

### Key Finding

AI appears in multiple curricula: AI in healthcare within DPT, AI in pharmacology/therapeutics within Pharm.D, an AI specialization in bioinformatics, and machine-learning/NLP/analytics courses in Health Informatics. These curricular appearances do not establish one national credential called “medical AI specialist.”

### Relevance to Learms

Model AI as a cross-cutting competency domain. Any user-facing title must come from the exact degree/track, not a generated occupational label.

### Citation

HEC. *DPT Curriculum*. 2025; HEC/PCP. *Pharm.D Curriculum*. 2025; HEC. *Bioinformatics Curricula*. 2026; HEC. *Computer Science Curricula*. 2025 (URLs in source register; accessed 2026-09-30).

## 7. Health Professions Education

### Source

CPSP current FCPS and MCPS program pages. [MED-031; MED-036]

### Key Finding

CPSP's current pages list **Health Professions Education** as both a first-fellowship label and an MCPS discipline. The two awards are not interchangeable; the FCPS page gives a general 4–5-year first-fellowship duration while the MCPS page states a two-year general duration.

### Relevance to Learms

Education-focused users need separate FCPS-HPE and MCPS-HPE pathways, current entry requirements, and accredited sites. Do not reduce HPE to a generic teaching skill tag.

### Citation

CPSP. *FCPS Training Programs*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/fcps.php; CPSP. *MCPS Training Programs*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/mcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 8. Health Care Systems Management

### Source

CPSP current MCPS program page. [MED-036]

### Key Finding

CPSP lists **Health Care Systems Management** among current MCPS disciplines and states that MCPS programs are generally two years. This is a named qualification route, distinct from a generic hospital-administration job title or a university business/management degree.

### Relevance to Learms

Store the CPSP award route and any university management degrees as different programs even if job outcomes overlap.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Membership of the College of Physicians and Surgeons—MCPS*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/mcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 9. Clinical research in pharmacy

### Source

HEC/PCP Pharm.D curriculum (2025). [MED-006]

### Key Finding

**Clinical Research in Pharmacy** is one of 12 recommended optional Pharm.D specialization clusters. It is a 15-credit curricular track where offered and approved; it is not by itself a national research-professional license.

### Relevance to Learms

Clinical-research learning content should be connected to research methods, ethics, protocol design, statistics, evidence appraisal, and data governance. Human-subject research permissions remain institution/legal/ethics-board matters.

### Citation

HEC and PCP. *Curriculum of Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D)*. 2025, p. 9. https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2024-2025/Pharm.D.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 10. Occupational therapy and newer curriculum development

### Source

HEC/AHPC occupational-therapy announcement (May 2026). [MED-015]

### Key Finding

HEC announced finalization of a Bachelor of Occupational Therapy curriculum with AHPC. The final booklet was not located during review. This illustrates that an emerging field can have an official announcement before a stable public curriculum artifact is available.

### Relevance to Learms

Use source states such as `announced`, `booklet_located`, `institution_offering_verified`, and `registration_route_verified`; avoid treating announcement as complete implementation evidence.

### Citation

HEC. *HEC Finalises Curriculum for Bachelor of Occupational Therapy Programme*. May 2026. https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/news/news/Pages/Therapy-Programme.aspx (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 11. Genetic counseling: evidence status

### Source

Reviewed AHPC discipline list, HEC 2026 Bioinformatics curriculum, and HEC revised-curricula index. [MED-013; MED-047]

### Key Finding

Genomics, pharmacogenomics, precision medicine, and clinical bioinformatics are present in the HEC curriculum. A nationally regulated Pakistan qualification or AHPC discipline explicitly titled **Genetic Counseling** was **not found** in the reviewed sources.

### Relevance to Learms

Do not create a “licensed genetic counselor” pathway for Pakistan from international role descriptions. Content may teach counseling concepts only with clear scope and jurisdiction warnings.

### Citation

AHPC. *Our Disciplines*. https://www.ahpc.org.pk/our-disciplines.html; HEC. *Bioinformatics Curricula*. 2026. https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2025-2026/Bioinformatics.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 12. Digital health and telemedicine: evidence status

### Source

HEC Computer Science and Public Health curricula. [MED-017; MED-048]

### Key Finding

Telehealth, mHealth, EHRs, interoperability, analytics, privacy, and security appear as educational content. A single national clinical qualification titled “Digital Health Specialist” was **not found** in the reviewed curriculum/regulator sources.

### Relevance to Learms

Treat digital health as a cross-disciplinary domain. Clinical authority remains with the user's regulated profession; technical competency does not expand clinical scope.

### Citation

HEC. *Computer Science Curricula*. 2025; HEC. *Public Health Curriculum*. 2025 (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 13. Data and AI safety implications

### Source

HEC Health Informatics and Public Health Informatics curriculum topics. [MED-017; MED-048]

### Key Finding

Both pathways explicitly include privacy/security or governance/ethics. Health AI education therefore cannot be reduced to model building; standards, interoperability, privacy, security, bias, validation, and responsible deployment are core adjacent competencies.

### Relevance to Learms

Agent 3 should require data minimization, access controls, provenance, auditability, de-identification, retention controls, and human review for health-data/AI learning features. Educational AI must not be presented as clinical decision authority.

### Citation

HEC. *Computer Science Curricula*. 2025, Health Informatics track; HEC. *Public Health Curriculum*. 2025, Public Health Informatics specialization (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 15. Demand, work-life, and earnings

### Source

Reviewed HEC, AHPC, CPSP, and PCP official sources. [MED-006; MED-013; MED-017; MED-031; MED-036; MED-047; MED-048]

### Key Finding

Official curricula demonstrate educational recognition of emerging domains but do not provide comparable Pakistan employment rates, vacancy forecasts, applicant ratios, working hours, or earnings. Those measures are **not found** for these fields.

### Relevance to Learms

Do not label a field “high demand” merely because a curriculum was introduced. Use separate evidence for program existence and labor-market demand.

### Citation

HEC curricula and relevant regulator program lists cited above (accessed 2026-09-30).
