# “Top” Medical Colleges in Pakistan — Evidence-Safe Comparison

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Conclusion:** A current official national league table of Pakistan medical colleges was **not found**. This file provides a comparison method, not an invented ranking.

## Topic

Whether an official current “top medical colleges” ranking exists.

### Source

HEC's official ranking page and archive, accessed 2026-09-30. [MED-060]

### Key Finding

HEC's official page links national ranking exercises for 2010, 2013, 2014, and 2015 and cautions that rankings reflect the research and quality criteria used in the exercise. No current 2025 or 2026 official medical-college league table was located on that page. Consequently, the 2015 archive must not be relabeled as a current “best medical colleges” list.

PM&DC's current directories identify recognized medical/dental college entries and seat allocations; they do not rank the entries. [MED-051–MED-054]

### Relevance to Learms

Learms should not publish one ordinal “Top 10” based on unsupported synthesis or commercial websites. It can offer source-grounded filters and let users select priorities.

### Citation

Higher Education Commission Pakistan. *Ranking of Pakistani HEIs*. Official ranking page and archive links through 2015. Accessed 2026-09-30. <https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/ranking/Pages/Ranking-of-Pakistani-HEIs.aspx>. MED-060.
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. Live college directories. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-051–MED-054.

## Topic

Why recognition, seats, hospitals, admission outcomes, and rankings answer different questions.

### Source

PM&DC directories [MED-051–MED-054], PM&DC hospital list [MED-055], PM&DC admissions regulations [MED-002], and HEC ranking page [MED-060].

### Key Finding

| Evidence | Question it can answer | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| PM&DC directory entry | Was this program-sector entry displayed by the regulator on the access date? | Rank, current intake permission, teaching quality, outcomes |
| PM&DC seat allocation | How many seats did the live regulator row display? | Applicant-specific chance, fill rate, competitiveness |
| PM&DC hospital list | Which own/affiliated hospitals were recorded at last inspection? | Case volume, supervision quality, current capacity |
| Admitting-authority selection list | Which candidates/aggregates were selected in a defined session/category/list round? | Future cutoff, educational quality, national rank |
| HEC historic ranking | How did an HEI score under that exercise's criteria and year? | Current medical-program rank or clinical training quality |
| College fee schedule | What did the institution publish as charges? | Legal permissibility unless checked against current PM&DC rules |

A university-level indicator may not represent one medical college or clinical program. Likewise, high historical closing merit may reflect applicant preferences, geography, quotas, or seat scarcity; it is not a validated educational-quality outcome.

### Relevance to Learms

Comparison cards should label the claim and source instead of compressing heterogeneous evidence into an unexplained score.

### Citation

PM&DC, official directories, admissions regulations, and attached-hospital list, MED-002 and MED-051–MED-055.
HEC Pakistan, official ranking page, MED-060.

## Topic

A defensible applicant-controlled shortlist.

### Source

PM&DC and admitting-authority evidence. [MED-002, MED-051–MED-055, MED-061–MED-063]

### Key Finding

A user can compare colleges without a league table by selecting and separately viewing:

1. **Eligibility and route:** exact program, session, domicile, open-merit/quota category, and admitting authority.
2. **Regulatory evidence:** current PM&DC directory entry and dated seat allocation.
3. **Clinical-training evidence:** named own/affiliated hospitals from the dated PM&DC list, while preserving pending-notification notes.
4. **Affordability:** PM&DC cap treatment, full college-published schedule, payment timing, refunds, hostel/transport, and non-tuition charges.
5. **Location constraints:** city, commute, hostel availability, family support, accessibility, and safety—using separately sourced data.
6. **Admissions evidence:** exact-session final selection lists for the same category; never mix provisional and final lists.
7. **User-defined preferences:** values such as location or cost should remain user weights, not be presented as objective quality weights.

**Not found in the reviewed official sources:** a current standardized national dataset comparing all colleges on graduation rates, licensing-exam outcomes, clinical case volume, student experience, research output at the medical-program level, accessibility, or graduate employment.

### Relevance to Learms

Agent 2 can implement a “Compare evidence” experience with no default overall score. Missing dimensions should display “not found,” not zero. Agent 1 can represent each metric as a dated observation with scope and provenance.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council, 2025 admissions regulations and current college/hospital directories, MED-002 and MED-051–MED-055.
University of Health Sciences, Khyber Medical University, and Dow University of Health Sciences official 2025–26 admissions/list portals, MED-061–MED-063.

## Topic

Claims that Learms should reject.

### Source

Evidence limitations identified above. [MED-002, MED-051–MED-055, MED-060–MED-063]

### Key Finding

Reject or qualify all of the following unless exact supporting evidence is attached:

- “College X is number one in Pakistan.”
- “More seats means better teaching.”
- “A listed hospital guarantees more clinical exposure.”
- “The highest cutoff proves the best outcomes.”
- “Recognition means admissions are currently open.”
- “An old HEC rank is the college's current rank.”
- “A university-wide international rank is the rank of its MBBS program.”

### Relevance to Learms

These rules prevent marketing language and stale rankings from entering generated guidance.

### Citation

Source set MED-002, MED-051–MED-055, and MED-060–MED-063; evidence cutoff 2026-09-30.
