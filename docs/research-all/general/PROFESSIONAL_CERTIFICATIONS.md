# Professional Bodies, Licensure Exams & Certifications in Pakistan

**Agent 4 — Area 1.3** · Last updated 2026-09-30

> **Why this matters more than the degree tree:** in Pakistan a *licence* often gates employment
> more tightly than a degree does. PEC registration, PM&DC registration, Bar Council enrolment and
> ICAP membership are all hard gates. Exam-prep for these gates is the highest-intent, highest-
> willingness-to-pay content Learms can carry, and the syllabi are **published, stable, and
> enumerable** — ideal for a knowledge graph.

## 1. ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants, UK)

### Topic: ACCA structure and difficulty profile
### Source: LearnSignal (2026); Eduyush (2026); PrepperGurukul (2026); BPP — all reporting ACCA Global's Dec-2025 results
### Key Finding

**13 exams across 3 levels + Ethics & Professional Skills Module (EPSM) + 36 months practical experience.**
Pass mark is **50% on every paper**.

| Level | Papers |
|---|---|
| Applied Knowledge (3) | BT Business & Technology · MA Management Accounting · FA Financial Accounting |
| Applied Skills (6) | LW Corporate & Business Law · PM Performance Management · TX Taxation · FR Financial Reporting · AA Audit & Assurance · FM Financial Management |
| Strategic Professional (4 = 2 essential + 2 of 4 optional) | **SBL**, **SBR** (essential) · AFM · APM · ATX · AAA (choose 2) |

**December 2025 pass rates** (this is the key signal for where Learms should invest tutoring depth):

| Paper | Level | Pass rate (Dec 2025) | Difficulty signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| BT | Knowledge | 87% | easiest entry point |
| MA | Knowledge | 64–72% ⚠ | sources disagree |
| FA | Knowledge | 66–68% ⚠ | sources disagree |
| LW | Skills | 82% | confidence-builder |
| TX | Skills | 52–55% ⚠ | |
| FR | Skills | 46–51% ⚠ | |
| FM | Skills | 48% | calculation-heavy |
| AA | Skills | 43–46% ⚠ | |
| **PM** | Skills | **40%** | hardest Applied Skill |
| SBL | Strategic | 50–51% | 4-hour integrated case study — longest exam |
| SBR | Strategic | 48% | |
| AFM | Strategic | 42–45% ⚠ | |
| ATX | Strategic | 40–50% ⚠ | large spread across sources |
| **APM** | Strategic | **36–41%** ⚠ | |
| **AAA** | Strategic | **32–38%** ⚠ | lowest pass rate in the qualification |

**[CONTESTED]** Secondary aggregators disagree on several papers by 3–8 percentage points (e.g. AAA
reported as both 32% and 38% for the *same* Dec-2025 sitting). **Do not hard-code these numbers.**
Treat the *ranking* as reliable (AAA/APM/PM hardest; BT/LW easiest) and the absolute values as
approximate. Verified figures must be pulled from ACCA Global's own "Pass rates" page.

Dec-2025 sitting had **120,633 exam entries** globally [Lakshya, 2026].

### Relevance to Learms
1. **Difficulty-weighted content priority**: PM, AA, FR, AFM, APM, AAA are where students actually
   fail and therefore where they will pay for help. BT/LW need only light coverage.
2. Recommended study order documented by multiple tutors: BT→MA→FA, then LW, then FR+AA together
   (content overlap), then PM/TX/FM, then SBL+SBR together. This is a **ready-made prerequisite
   DAG** for Agent 1's knowledge graph — the FR→SBR and PM→APM and AA→AAA edges are explicit.
3. The 4 exam sittings per year (March, June, September, December) give Learms a natural
   **4-cycle study-plan engine** with hard deadlines — excellent for spaced-repetition scheduling
   and for retention hooks.

## 2. CA — Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan (ICAP)

### Topic: ICAP CA qualification structure, attrition, and training contract
### Source: ICAP official FAQs; IFAC SMO Action Plan — Pakistan/ICAP (2020); ProPakistani; industry commentary (2026)
### Key Finding

ICAP's CA has **four levels** (IES-6 compliant, confirmed in ICAP's own IFAC filing):
**AFC → CAF → CFAP → MSA**, where AFC was replaced by **PRC** (Pre-Requisite Competencies) under the
**Education Scheme 2021**.

| Level | Name | Papers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AFC / **PRC** (Education Scheme 2021) | 4 (AFC) — PRC restructured | AFC1 Functional English & AFC2 Business Communication both map to PRC 1 "Business Writing and Comprehension Skills"; AFC3 Quantitative Methods → PRC Quantitative Methods |
| 2 | **CAF** Certificate in Accounting and Finance | 8 or 9 ⚠ | ICAP's own FAQ header says "**CAF – 8 papers**"; ProPakistani (2020, pre-2021 scheme) says 9 |
| 3 | **CFAP** Certified Finance and Accounting Professional | 6 | max 6 attempts per paper; unlimited after passing 3 |
| 4 | **MSA** Multi-Subject Assessment | 2 | case-study / scenario based; unlimited attempts |

**Hard rules worth encoding:**
- **Attempt limits.** AFC: 3 attempts per paper (+2 extra for remaining papers if 2 of 4 passed in 3 attempts). CFAP: 6 attempts per paper; unlimited once 3 papers passed. MSA: unlimited. [ProPakistani/ICAP]
- **Time limit.** Must pass ≥4 CFAP papers within **10 years** of commencing training. [ICAP FAQ]
- **CAF group rule.** Must pass ≥2 papers of CAF Group A before appearing in Group B papers. [ICAP FAQ]
- **CFAP eligibility.** Must complete **12 months of training** before sitting CFAP. [ICAP FAQ]
- **Training contract (articleship):** 3, 3.5 or 4 years depending on entry qualification [IFAC/ICAP, 2020]; widely reported as **42 months** for the common route.
- **Entry:** HSSC ≥50% aggregate, or A-Level with ≥2 passes, or graduate (14/16 years) with ≥45%. [ICAP]
- **Exemptions:** full ICMAP pass → all CAF papers exempt; full PIPFA pass → CAF-4 Business Law + CAF-7 Company Law exempt. [ICAP FAQ]
- **Pass rates: ~25–35% overall, some papers <20%.** **[MED / CONTESTED]** — ICAP does not publish
  detailed paper-level statistics, so all circulating figures are secondary/industry estimates.
  Realistic completion timeline 5–8 years. [ictbusinessschool, 2026; kamranrashid, 2026]

### Relevance to Learms
- ICAP CA is **the highest-attrition mainstream qualification in Pakistan**. A product that
  measurably raises CFAP/MSA pass probability has an obvious, quantifiable value proposition.
- The attempt limits and the 10-year CFAP window create *genuine deadline anxiety* — Learms'
  planner and wellness modules should both be aware of `attempts_used` / `attempts_remaining`.
- CAF Group A→B dependency and the ICMAP/PIPFA exemption table are **graph edges**, not prose.
- **Gap:** ICAP does not publish paper-level pass rates. Learms cannot claim precise difficulty
  numbers for CA the way it can for ACCA. Say "historically low" and cite the range.

## 4. Law — Pakistan Bar Council (PBC) + HEC Education Testing Council

### Topic: The licensure path to becoming an advocate in Pakistan
### Source: HEC/ETC and PBC rules as reported by Adalat Online (2026), Access to Justice (2026), Legal Academy PK
### Key Finding

Two gates, both run by HEC's Education Testing Council under PBC rules:

| Gate | When | What it does | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LAT** (Law Admission Test) | *Before* enrolling | Required for admission to the 5-year LLB. Subjects: English, Essay Writing, General Knowledge, Islamic Studies, etc. | varies |
| **Law-GAT** (Law Graduate Assessment Test) | *After* LLB | Mandatory for enrolment as an Advocate with any provincial Bar Council | **50%**, no negative marking |

**Law-GAT specifics:**
- Eligibility: completed LLB (3-yr or 5-yr) from an HEC- **and** PBC-recognised university; result-awaiting candidates are **not** eligible; ≥50% / equivalent CGPA in LLB; no age limit; overseas Pakistanis eligible.
- Held **3 times a year**.
- Syllabus: Constitution of Pakistan · Jurisprudence · Civil Law · Criminal Law · Law of Evidence (Qanun-e-Shahadat) · English & Legal Reasoning · General Knowledge · International Law (UN Charter, ICJ Statute).
- **[CONTESTED] Attempt limit: 5 vs 7.** One 2026 source states 5 maximum attempts; another states "per the latest notification, 07 attempts". Learms must not state a number without a dated PBC/HEC notification. Show it as "limited attempts — check the current HEC notification".

### Relevance to Learms
- Law-GAT is a **closed, enumerable, high-stakes syllabus with a fixed 50% pass mark** — the single
  easiest law product to build well. Item bank + 7-topic blueprint + 3 sittings/year.
- The 3-sittings-a-year cadence plus attempt limits again argue for deadline-aware planning.
- **Do not** present attempt limits as fact until verified (see above).

## 5. Medicine — PM&DC and the MDCAT

### Topic: MDCAT structure, weighting and thresholds
### Source: PM&DC 2025 Uniform MDCAT Curriculum (primary, hosted by KMU); secondary explainers 2025–2026
### Key Finding

**Primary-source facts (PM&DC "2025 Medical and Dental Colleges Admission Test (MDCAT) Curriculum"):**
- **180 MCQs · 3 hours · paper-based · no negative marking**
- Minimum for **medical (MBBS) = 55%**; **dental (BDS) = 50%**
- Difficulty mix: **15% easy / 70% moderate / 15% hard**
- Within Biology, Chemistry and Physics: **70% recall / 30% application-level** questions

**Subject weighting (this is the content budget for any MDCAT product):**

| Subject | % | MCQs |
|---|---|---|
| **Biology** | 45% | 81 |
| Chemistry | 25% | 45 |
| Physics | 20% | 36 |
| English | 5% | 9 |
| Logical Reasoning | 5% | 9 |

**[CONTESTED] Eligibility threshold.** Sources give the FSc Pre-Medical minimum as 55%, 60%, or 65%.
The most recent and most specific claim is **65% for the 2025-26 session, raised from 60%**
[parhlai, 2026]. One outlier source inverts the MDCAT pass marks entirely (claiming 60% MBBS / 55% BDS
out of 200) — that contradicts the PM&DC curriculum document and should be **discarded**.
**Rule: trust the PM&DC PDF over every aggregator.**

### Relevance to Learms
1. **Biology is 45% of the paper.** Any MDCAT product that allocates content effort evenly across
   subjects is mis-allocated by a factor of ~2. Effort should mirror 45/25/20/5/5.
2. **70% recall / 30% application** is a decisive instructional-design fact: MDCAT rewards
   *retrieval practice and spaced repetition* far more than it rewards deep Socratic problem-solving.
   For MDCAT specifically, Learms should weight FSRS flashcards heavily and Socratic dialogue lightly.
   (Contrast with ECAT/engineering, which is problem-solving heavy.)
3. The 15/70/15 difficulty distribution is a ready-made **item-difficulty target** for the item bank
   and can calibrate an IRT/Elo difficulty scale.
4. Merit is an **aggregate** of MDCAT + FSc + Matric — the product should model aggregate, not raw score.

## 6. Engineering — Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC)

### Key Finding
- PEC accredits engineering programmes and registers engineers; graduating from a **non-accredited**
  programme materially damages employability and blocks registration.
- PEC publishes curricula in cohorts: **2020** (13 disciplines), **2023** (5), **2024** (7) — see
  `PAKISTAN_FIELDS_TAXONOMY.md` §2.3 for the full list.
- PEC maintains **Level-I vs Level-II** accreditation lists per programme per intake batch. Level-II
  corresponds to the outcome-based-education (Washington Accord aligned) regime. Accreditation is
  granted **per intake batch**, not permanently — so "is my batch accredited?" is a real,
  frequently-asked student question.

### Relevance to Learms
- Store accreditation at `(university, programme, intake_batch)` granularity, because PEC does.
- A simple "is my batch PEC Level-II accredited?" lookup would be a genuinely useful, cheap,
  differentiated feature — the data is public but painfully presented on pec.org.pk.

- https://www.pec.org.pk/curriculum-fee-structure/all-curriculums/
- https://www.pec.org.pk/accredition/programs-under-level-1/
