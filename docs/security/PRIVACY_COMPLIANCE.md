# Privacy and compliance

Classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted. Student identity, learning, wellness, credentials, and recovery material are Restricted. Collect only what a feature needs; obtain granular informed consent, record withdrawal, and honor deletion/export requests subject to documented legal retention. Default analytics are aggregated and pseudonymized. Retention schedules are approved by the data owner and automatic deletion is monitored.

Design for GDPR principles, Pakistan privacy-law requirements as enacted/applicable, COPPA safeguards for under-13 users, and FERPA-like education controls. Obtain guardian consent where required. Breach response preserves evidence, contains access, assesses impact, and follows applicable notification duties; this is not legal advice.
