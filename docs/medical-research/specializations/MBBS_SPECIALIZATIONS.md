# MBBS Specializations and Postgraduate Pathways in Pakistan

**Evidence cutoff:** 30 September 2026  
**Scope:** Pakistan; MBBS-accessible medical, surgical, diagnostic, public-health, and academic pathways  
**Primary authorities:** PM&DC for recognition/registration; CPSP for FCPS/MCPS programs and accredited training  
**Status:** Source-grounded pathway map, not admission or career advice

> **Safety rule:** A specialty name on a CPSP page is not proof that a particular hospital, intake, qualification, or training seat is recognized. Verify the qualification, awarding body, program, training site, supervisor, intake/session, and PM&DC registration status at the time of application.

## 1. What “specialization” means in this file

Learms must not collapse the following into one field:

1. **Base qualification:** MBBS.
2. **FCPS-I examination group:** one of CPSP's broad screening disciplines.
3. **First fellowship:** a primary specialist pathway.
4. **Second fellowship:** a post-fellowship subspecialist pathway.
5. **University qualification:** MD, MS, MPhil, diploma, or another named award.
6. **Specialty/discipline:** the domain of practice or study.
7. **Accredited training site:** institution and specialty-specific approval.
8. **Regulator registration:** whether the resulting additional qualification can be entered by PM&DC.
9. **Job role:** resident, medical officer, specialist, consultant, faculty member, researcher, or another role.

The same clinical domain can have different award routes. Conversely, a similarly named qualification can have different recognition status by training site and cohort. [MED-001; MED-031; MED-033; MED-039; MED-040]

## 2. Governing recognition logic

### Source

PM&DC, *Postgraduate Medical/Dental Education Standards and Regulations* (2023); PM&DC, *Recognized Pakistani Postgraduate Qualifications as of 18 August 2026*; PM&DC, postgraduate recognition clarification (27 February 2026). [MED-042; MED-039; MED-040]

### Key Finding

PM&DC's 2023 framework distinguishes postgraduate levels and states that approved clinical FCPS/MD-type programs commonly involve 4–5 years with a research component. The live 2026 register maps qualification title, training institute, and awarding institution. PM&DC's 2026 clarification says post-16 January 2023 entrants must satisfy recognition of the qualification, awarding institution, and training site; historic amnesty rules are not a general substitute for recognition.

### Relevance to Learms

A search result for “MD Cardiology” or “FCPS Cardiology” must not return a blanket “recognized” badge. Recognition is a versioned relation among qualification, program, site, awarder, induction date, and regulator record.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Postgraduate Medical/Dental Education Standards and Regulations*. 2023. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/law/Approved%20PG%20regulations%202023.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *Recognized Pakistani PGQs 18.08.2026*. 2026. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Others/Recognized%20Pakistani%20PGQs%2018.08.2026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *PMDC gives relief to MD/MS/PhD degree holders/students*. 27 February 2026. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/press/pg%20press%20release%20md%20ms%20mds%20fcps01032026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 3. FCPS pathway skeleton

### Source

CPSP current FCPS program page; CPSP National Residency Program booklet; CPSP training guidelines. [MED-031; MED-033; MED-034]

### Key Finding

The current CPSP program page states that first-fellowship training varies from **4–5 years**. The National Residency Program describes the usual sequence as:

`MBBS + registration/house job → FCPS-I or exemption → accredited institutional induction → RTMC registration → supervised core/rotational/specialty training → IMM where prescribed → FCPS-II requirements and examination → fellowship`

The NRP says most first-fellowship pathways comprise two core years followed by two or three advanced years. It describes second fellowship as post-first-fellowship training of two years, but the current program page must be consulted for live offerings and specialty-specific rules. Training guidelines require prompt RTMC registration, an approved supervisor and institution, rotations, mandatory learning activities, e-logbook evidence, and a dissertation or eligible research papers.

### Relevance to Learms

Learms should display a dependency graph, not a flat “degree after MBBS” card. Each requirement needs `required`, `conditional`, `historical`, and `verify_current` states.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Fellowship of the College of Physicians and Surgeons—FCPS*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *National Residency Program*. 2022. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/dnrp/DNRP_Booklet.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *General Information: Post Graduate Training and Their Requirements*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/general-info.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 6. Alternate route: university MD/MS and other postgraduate awards

### Source

PM&DC postgraduate standards and the 18 August 2026 recognized-qualification register. [MED-042; MED-039]

### Key Finding

Pakistan also has university-awarded MD, MS, MPhil, MPH/MSPH, and diploma pathways. The current PM&DC register includes many institution-specific examples across medicine, paediatrics, psychiatry, cardiology, nephrology, diagnostic radiology, general and specialty surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, basic sciences, and public health. The valid unit is the exact row in the PM&DC register—not the degree acronym in isolation.

### Relevance to Learms

Search facets should include `award_type`, `specialty`, `awarding_institution`, `training_site`, `recognition_as_of`, and `cohort_rule`. FCPS and MD/MS may occupy a similar postgraduate level but are not the same award.

### Citation

Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. *Postgraduate Medical/Dental Education Standards and Regulations*. 2023. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/law/Approved%20PG%20regulations%202023.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *Recognized Pakistani PGQs 18.08.2026*. 2026. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Others/Recognized%20Pakistani%20PGQs%2018.08.2026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 7. MCPS is a distinct route

### Source

CPSP current MCPS program page. [MED-036]

### Key Finding

The current page states that MCPS training is **two years** and lists 19 disciplines: Anaesthesiology, Clinical Pathology, Community Medicine, Operative Dentistry, Orthodontics, Periodontology, Prosthodontics, Diagnostic Radiology, Family Dentistry, Family Medicine, Forensic Medicine, Health Care Systems Management, Health Professions Education, Military Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Ophthalmology, ENT, Paediatrics, and Psychiatry.

### Relevance to Learms

MCPS must not be rendered as an abbreviation or “short FCPS.” It is a separate qualification route with its own disciplines, training, assessment, and PM&DC recognition checks.

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Membership of the College of Physicians and Surgeons—MCPS*. https://listing.cpsp.edu.pk/weblink_new/training_program/mcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 8. Entry requirements: what is verified

### Source

CPSP NRP booklet; CPSP current training guidelines; PM&DC recognized-PG register. [MED-033; MED-034; MED-039]

### Key Finding

The general FCPS pathway requires an eligible MBBS/BDS qualification and professional registration, house job or the stated recognized alternative, FCPS-I or exemption, induction into an available accredited slot, RTMC registration, and specialty-specific supervised training. Institutional selection remains separate from passing FCPS-I: the NRP says selection depends on the accredited institution's process and available slots.

### Relevance to Learms

The UI must separate:

- `eligible_to_take_screening_exam`
- `passed_screening_exam`
- `eligible_to_apply_for_training`
- `selected_by_training_institution`
- `RTMC_registered`
- `qualification_program_site_PMDC_recognized`

A passed FCPS-I result is not a guaranteed training seat.

### Citation

CPSP. *National Residency Program*. 2022. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/dnrp/DNRP_Booklet.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *General Information: Post Graduate Training and Their Requirements*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/general-info.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 9. Training and assessment components

### Source

CPSP NRP booklet and training guidelines. [MED-033; MED-034]

### Key Finding

Documented components include competency-based curricula, supervised core and specialty rotations, daily e-logbook monitoring, formative/workplace-based assessment, mandatory workshops, research, IMM where prescribed, and a final FCPS-II written and clinical assessment. CPSP's web guidelines say trainees must submit a synopsis within the prescribed period and complete a dissertation or eligible research-paper route.

### Relevance to Learms

A useful learning workspace needs:

- rotation-specific competency plans;
- cases/procedures with supervisor sign-off, without patient identifiers;
- IMM and FCPS-II blueprints kept distinct;
- research milestones;
- workshop and expiry reminders;
- evidence snapshots by registration cohort.

### Citation

CPSP. *National Residency Program*. 2022. https://elogbook.cpsp.edu.pk/eportal/eportal/docs/dnrp/DNRP_Booklet.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30); CPSP. *General Information: Post Graduate Training and Their Requirements*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/general-info.php (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 10. Specialty-specific curriculum coverage is incomplete on the public index

### Source

CPSP clinical-guidelines index and current fellowship list. [MED-031; MED-035]

### Key Finding

The clinical-guidelines index links guidance for major subjects and many other disciplines, but it does not mirror all labels on the current 98-specialty statement. Newer second-fellowship labels such as colorectal surgery, interventional radiology, palliative medicine, spine surgery, and transplant nephrology are not visible in the reviewed guideline index.

### Relevance to Learms

Absence of a public PDF must be represented as `curriculum_publicly_located = false`, not `program_does_not_exist`. Learms should show source coverage separately from program status.

| Issue | Source A | Source B | Safe interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total fellowships | Current FCPS page says **98** [MED-031] | Same page visibly enumerates 48 first + 44 second = **92** | Do not publish a computed definitive total; store statement and enumerated count separately and request CPSP clarification |
| Accredited-directory count | Current FCPS page contains newer labels [MED-031] | Institution selector exposes 78 visible specialty labels [MED-032] | Directory taxonomy may lag; verify specialty-site pairing directly |
| First/second route | Current page allows selected medicine/surgery allied specialties via first or second fellowship [MED-031] | Older NRP pathway tables encode a smaller set [MED-033] | Version route rules by effective date and specialty |

| Public examination guidance | Current program list has 92 enumerated labels [MED-031] | Public clinical-guideline index covers fewer [MED-035] | Missing public guideline is an evidence gap, not a nonexistent program |

## 12. Competitiveness, demand, work-life, and earnings

### Source

CPSP and PM&DC official sources reviewed above. [MED-031–MED-040]

### Key Finding

A national, specialty-by-specialty official dataset for applicant-to-seat ratios, cutoff scores, fill rates, working hours, wellbeing, public/private vacancies, or earnings was **not found** in the reviewed official sources. CPSP identifies available accredited institutions and the general selection process, but not a comparable national competitiveness index. PM&DC's recognition register does not report labor-market outcomes.

### Relevance to Learms

Do not generate “easy/hard,” “high demand,” “best work-life,” or salary rankings from anecdotes. These facets should remain `not_found` until supported by dated, geographically specific workforce, vacancy, payroll, or survey evidence. User-reported data, if later collected, must be labelled self-reported and never presented as regulator data.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *FCPS Accredited Institutions*. https://www.cpsp.edu.pk/accredited-institutes-fcps.php (accessed 2026-09-30); PM&DC. *Recognized Pakistani PGQs 18.08.2026*. https://pmdc.pk/Documents/Others/Recognized%20Pakistani%20PGQs%2018.08.2026.pdf (accessed 2026-09-30).

## 14. UI requirements

1. **Pathway timeline:** base degree → house job/registration → exam → institutional selection → registered training → intermediate/final assessment.
2. **Recognition banner:** exact PM&DC record and “checked on” date.
3. **Source disagreement badge:** display both the stated CPSP total and enumerated-page count without silently resolving it.
4. **Specialty compare:** compare documented curriculum, assessment, and pathway—not unsourced prestige, lifestyle, or salary.
5. **Current-source links:** route users to CPSP's program and accredited-institution directories and PM&DC's current register.
6. **No false guarantees:** wording should say “may be eligible to apply,” never “will obtain a seat.”
7. **Safety:** do not store patient names, record numbers, images, dates, or case narratives in learning logs unless a compliant clinical system and lawful workflow is separately established.
