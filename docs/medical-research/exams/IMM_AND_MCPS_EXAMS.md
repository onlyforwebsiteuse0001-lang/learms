# CPSP Intermediate Module (IMM) and MCPS Examinations

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30

## Topic

IMM's role in FCPS training.

### Source

CPSP National Residency Program booklet and exam FAQ. [MED-033, MED-080]

### Key Finding

IMM is a mid-residency assessment for prescribed FCPS programs, not a standalone specialist qualification. CPSP's NRP describes eligibility after two years in the core specialty, required workshops, synopsis submission, RTMC registration, and completed training/log evidence. The assessment includes theory and TOACS. [MED-033]

The FAQ states that applicable residents must pass IMM theory and TOACS within six consecutive attempts, availed or unavailed, from completion of two years in training. [MED-080]

### Relevance to Learms

Graph IMM as an assessment milestone linked to specific FCPS programs. Do not place it beside FCPS or MCPS as an independent terminal credential.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *National Residency Program Booklet*. 2022. MED-033.
College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Exam FAQ*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-080.

## Topic

MCPS pathway and duration.

### Source

CPSP current MCPS program page and NRP booklet. [MED-033, MED-036]

### Key Finding

CPSP's current page lists 19 MCPS disciplines and states a two-year training duration. The NRP booklet's broad eligibility model includes MBBS, or BDS for dental specialties, one year of recognized house job, then two years of RTMC-registered training in an approved unit under CPSP requirements. [MED-033, MED-036]

MCPS is not “FCPS Part I,” not the IMM, and not automatically interchangeable with an MD/MS/MDS program. Qualification, program, site, and PM&DC registration remain separate claims.

### Relevance to Learms

Build a distinct MCPS path: basic qualification -> house job -> selection/RTMC training -> workshops/logbook -> MCPS theory -> clinical/TOACS -> election/qualification -> PM&DC qualification registration.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Membership Training Programs — MCPS*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-036.
College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *National Residency Program Booklet*. 2022. MED-033.

## Topic

MCPS examination pattern.

### Source

CPSP MCPS examination guidelines and NRP booklet. [MED-081, MED-033]

### Key Finding

CPSP's general guideline shows specialty-dependent theory patterns. Some named disciplines use Paper I with 10 SAQs and Paper II with 100 MCQs; another listed group uses 10 SAQs in each paper. Each paper is three hours in the conventional format. Candidates successful in theory are then informed about oral/clinical examination, and the guideline says clinical examinations are tentatively scheduled three to four weeks after theory. [MED-081]

The guideline's specialty rows include overlaps and labels that do not exactly match the current 19-discipline program page. Therefore, the current specialty prospectus and sitting notification are necessary; the table should not be used to infer a pattern solely from a similar label.

### Relevance to Learms

Assessment format belongs to a `(qualification, discipline, document version)` record, not globally to MCPS.

### Citation

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *MCPS Examination Guidelines*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-081.

## Topic

2026 calendar.

### Source

CPSP official examinations schedule. [MED-078]

### Key Finding

CPSP lists IMM theory on 16 February and 4 August 2026, and MCPS theory on 10 February and 4 August 2026. The public schedule does not establish every discipline's later clinical/TOACS date.

### Relevance to Learms

Store one event per component and sitting. Past dates should remain as evidence history but never be displayed as upcoming.

## Topic

Recognition after passing.

### Source

CPSP FAQ and PM&DC postgraduate qualification register. [MED-039, MED-080]

### Key Finding

CPSP's FAQ says successful candidates are sent to the College Council for election as Fellows/Members and then receive an election letter/provisional certificate; PM&DC registration of the qualification is a separate step. PM&DC's current register must be checked for qualification/program/training-site recognition. [MED-039, MED-080]

### Relevance to Learms

“Passed exam,” “elected Member,” and “additional qualification registered by PM&DC” are three statuses.

College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan. *Exam FAQ*. MED-080.
