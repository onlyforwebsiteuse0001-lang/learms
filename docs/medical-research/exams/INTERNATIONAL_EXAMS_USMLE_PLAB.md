# International Examination Pathways — USMLE/ECFMG and PLAB/GMC

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-30
**Scope:** High-level official pathway map for Pakistan-trained medical graduates; not immigration, employment, or individualized licensing advice

## Topic

United States examination and ECFMG certification boundary.

### Source

USMLE official step-exam site and ECFMG certification overview/2026 requirements. [MED-082, MED-083]

### Key Finding

USMLE has Step 1, Step 2 Clinical Knowledge (CK), and Step 3. For an international medical graduate (IMG), ECFMG certification is a separate process. ECFMG says its medical-science requirement requires passing Step 1 and Step 2 CK. Its clinical/communication requirement requires an accepted ECFMG Pathway including satisfactory OET Medicine, or a still-valid former Step 2 CS pass. The medical school must have an ECFMG Sponsor Note in the World Directory covering the applicant's graduation year. [MED-082, MED-083]

ECFMG certification is required for IMGs entering ACGME-accredited residency/fellowship and is one eligibility requirement for IMG access to Step 3; it is not itself a residency match, visa, state license, or job. [MED-082]

### Relevance to Learms

Do not present “USMLE passed” as one status. Model exam components, ECFMG school eligibility, credentials verification, Pathway/OET, certification, residency application/match, Step 3, state licensing, and immigration separately.

### Citation

Intealth/ECFMG. *ECFMG Certification Overview* and *2026 Information Booklet — Requirements for ECFMG Certification*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-082.
United States Medical Licensing Examination. *Step Exams*. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-083.

## Topic

ECFMG pathway time sensitivity.

### Source

ECFMG certification overview and 2026 requirements. [MED-082]

### Key Finding

ECFMG states that Pathways operate on seasonal application cycles and accepted Pathways can expire. It also applies a seven-year framework to completion of examination requirements in the 2026 information. Applicants must use the edition current to their application and verify that school/graduation-year eligibility and Pathway validity remain current. [MED-082]

### Relevance to Learms

A timeline tool must version ECFMG cycle, Pathway type, acceptance/expiration, exam dates, and credentials status. Never use a timeless checklist copied from a prior Match cycle.

### Citation

Intealth/ECFMG. *ECFMG Certification Overview*; *2026 Information Booklet*. MED-082.

## Topic

UK PLAB pathway.

### Source

General Medical Council official PLAB guide. [MED-084]

### Key Finding

GMC describes PLAB as two parts. PLAB 1 is a written three-hour examination with 180 single-best-answer questions. PLAB 2 is an OSCE with 16 scenarios, each eight minutes. Before booking PLAB 1, a candidate needs an acceptable overseas primary medical qualification, acceptable English evidence, and a GMC Online account. After passing both parts, the candidate may apply for registration with a licence to practise; GMC says the application should be approved within two years of passing PLAB 2. [MED-084]

Passing PLAB does not itself grant registration, a job, a training number, a visa, or specialist recognition.

### Relevance to Learms

Represent English evidence, qualification acceptability, PLAB 1, PLAB 2, registration application, licence, employment, and immigration as separate stages.

### Citation

General Medical Council. *A Guide to the PLAB Test* and PLAB portal. Accessed 2026-09-30. MED-084.

## Topic

Local licensing remains separate.

### Source

PM&DC licensing and NRE law plus ECFMG/GMC sources. [MED-064, MED-066, MED-073, MED-074, MED-082–MED-084]

### Key Finding

USMLE/ECFMG and PLAB/GMC pathways do not replace PM&DC provisional/full registration or NRE requirements for practice in Pakistan. Conversely, PM&DC registration does not automatically establish U.S. or UK eligibility. Each authority verifies its own degree, school, examination, recency, good-standing, and registration requirements.

### Relevance to Learms

Never create equivalence edges such as `FCPS = U.S. board certification`, `PMDC license = GMC registration`, or `NRE = PLAB`. Use jurisdiction-specific credential graphs.

### Citation

PM&DC sources MED-064, MED-066, MED-073, and MED-074; ECFMG/USMLE/GMC sources MED-082–MED-084.
